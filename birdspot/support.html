BirdSpot · Privacy policy

Respect the birds: disturbing wild birds or destroying their nests is forbidden. Watch from a distance, with no playback lures or bait, do not approach a nest or a chick and do not use drones where they are restricted. The AI can be wrong: it is not a field guide.

BirdSpot tells you which birds you can see in your area today, identifies the bird in a photo with AI, shows bird hides and bird protection areas on a 3D satellite map, and keeps your sightings in a journal with your life list. Here are the most common questions.

Frequently asked questions

How do I identify a bird?

On the Today screen, tap "Identify" and then "Take photo" or "Choose from gallery". Try to get the whole bird in the frame, with the beak and plumage clearly visible; if it is far away, use the zoom and do not move closer. The app gives you the most likely species with a confidence percentage, the visible features, the look-alikes and how to tell them apart, its protection status in Spain and a tip to watch it without disturbing it. If the confidence is low, the app says so: do not rely on that result.

Why does it ask me to watch a video before identifying?

In the free version, a short full-screen video is shown before each identification; BirdSpot Pro removes it, and the banner too. If the video is not available or you close it before it ends, the photo is not identified: try again or go Pro. With or without Pro, there is a limit of 40 identifications per day per account.

The Today list is empty or very short

The birds come from public GBIF records for a cell of about 25 km around where you are (or where you left the map if you did not give location permission). In areas with few observers the list is short; move the map to another area or identify a bird yourself. Offline, the app shows what it last loaded.

What do "Passing through", "Arriving now", "Resident" and "Winter visitor" mean?

This is what is typical for the month in your area, worked out from the records: a resident lives there all year, a winter visitor arrives in autumn and spends the winter, a summer visitor arrives in spring and spends the summer, a bird passing through only goes by on its way elsewhere and one arriving now is just starting to be seen. They are indicative.

Who can see where I saw a bird?

When you save a sighting you choose: only me (by default), my friends or everyone. The photo, species, note and date show in the feed; the place, only as you choose. Nests, chicks and endangered species are not published and their place is yours alone; for vulnerable ones, if you share the place, it is blurred to about 10 km.

I saved a sighting and it does not show in the feed

If the photo was of a nest, a chick or an endangered species, only you see it, to protect the birds. A sighting that collects 3 reports is also hidden.

I found a nest, a chick or an injured bird

Do not touch it or go closer. If a bird is injured or seems abandoned, in Spain call 112 or your region's wildlife service: a chick on the ground usually has its parents nearby. Do not post it with a location.

How do I add friends?

The Friends tab shows your 6-character code: share it or type your friend's in "Add by code". You are friends only when the other person accepts the request. From there you also see and filter the feed: All, Friends or Mine.

How does "Go" work?

From a spot, a bird hide or a sighting with a place, "Go" opens a map with a line to the place and a compass with bearing and distance. On a Mac, which has no compass, it gives you bearing and distance. "Directions by car" opens Apple Maps.

I bought Pro and it does not show

Go to Friends → ⚙ → See BirdSpot Pro → Restore purchases. Sometimes the store takes a few minutes to confirm the payment. If it still does not show, write to us with your Apple receipt.

How do I cancel the Pro subscription?

It is managed from your Apple account: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → BirdSpot Pro.

What if a sighting or a spot is inappropriate?

Tap "More options" on its page and choose "Report" (dangerous information, offensive content, spam or another reason). When it collects 3 reports it is hidden automatically and the developer reviews it within 24 hours. You can also block its author so you no longer see their sightings.

How do I delete my account?

In the Friends tab, tap the ⚙ gear and "Delete my account". Your name, sightings with their photos and locations, spots, friendships and reports and blocks are deleted. It cannot be undone.

The app says "Update BirdSpot"

There is a new version with important changes. Tap "Open the App Store" and update to keep using it.

Where the data comes from

  • Map and satellite imagery: Apple Maps (MapKit).
  • Bird hides and observation towers: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL licence).
  • SPA and Natura 2000: © European Union (DG ENV) and European Environment Agency (EEA), CC BY 4.0. It is general information: the official boundaries are held by the competent authorities.
  • Birds of each area: records from GBIF.org and its publishers (CC0 and CC BY).
  • Bird names: Wikidata (CC0).
  • Protection in Spain: the Spanish Catalogue of Threatened Species (Royal Decree 139/2011) and Law 42/2007, from the BOE.
  • Bird illustrations: BirdSpot's own.
