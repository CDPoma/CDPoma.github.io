Privacy policy

Help & support

Summary: Níscalo identifies mushrooms from a photo using artificial intelligence and lets you publish your finds on a public wall. You can identify without an account, but to publish or identify you need an account, which no longer uses email or a sign-in key: it is only a display name you choose. What you publish (photo, description, probable species and, if you allow it, the GPS location) is public to anyone, even without an account. The app shows Google AdMob ads, and the Pro subscription removes them. Usage statistics are anonymous.

Who is responsible

Gabriel Micó Victoria, developer of Níscalo. For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights, write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Account

To identify, publish, report or block, you need an account. The account no longer has an email or a sign-in key: it is an anonymous Supabase session with only a display name that you choose and that other people see on your posts. We recommend a nickname rather than your full name. If you delete the app, you start from scratch, and you can delete your account in Settings → Delete my account. Your account is stored in Supabase, with servers in the European Union (Frankfurt).

Identifying a mushroom

When you tap «Identify», the photo is sent to our server and from there to Anthropic's artificial intelligence service (Claude). If that service does not respond, the photo is sent to Google's service (Gemini) for the same task. It is only used to give you the identification.

  • We do not keep the photo or the result unless you publish the find.
  • We count how many identifications each account makes per day, to apply the usage limit. That counter does not include the photo.

Publishing a find

When you publish, we store the photo, the description (optional), the probable species returned by the identification and, if you grant location permission, the GPS position of the find. All of this is public: anyone can see it, also without an account, on the wall and on the map.

  • Without location permission, the app warns you and you can publish without location, if you confirm.
  • Do not publish photos of people or data you do not want everyone to see. The location of a mushroom may reveal where you forage.

Reporting and blocking

You can report any post and block its author. Blocking hides their posts from you. A post that collects 3 reports is hidden automatically and kept for review by the developer.

Deleting your data

  • One post: delete it from its page. It is removed together with its photo.
  • Your whole account: Settings → Delete my account. Your profile, posts, photos, reports, blocks and usage counter are deleted. The sign-in account is deleted too, unless you use it in another of our apps.

Ads and purchases

The app shows Google AdMob ads (a banner and a rewarded video you watch before the photo is analysed). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google asks for your consent before showing ads; you can change it in the same form. On iPhone, Apple asks whether you allow tracking (ATT); if you do not, ads are not personalised with that tracking.

Pro is a subscription managed by Apple's App Store, with RevenueCat to verify your purchase. Pro removes the ads. Apple handles the payment; we only learn whether the subscription is active.

Usage statistics

We use Firebase Analytics anonymously to see which screens are used and improve the app. It is only enabled according to your consent (the choice you make in Google's form). We do not send your email, name, location or the text of your finds, and the app does not use the iPhone advertising identifier.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction or deletion by writing to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com, although you can already do almost everything from the app itself.

Safety warning: Níscalo does not replace the opinion of an expert mycologist. No photo-based identification is infallible. Do not eat any mushroom if you have the slightest doubt, and always check with an expert before consuming any specimen.
