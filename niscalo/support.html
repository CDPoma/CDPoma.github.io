Help & support

Privacy policy

Safety first: Níscalo is an aid, not an expert. Do not eat any mushroom if you have the slightest doubt, even if the app says it is edible. Always check with an expert mycologist before eating any specimen.

Níscalo identifies mushrooms from a photo using artificial intelligence, and lets you publish your finds, with photo, description and location, on a public wall. Something not working, or an idea? Write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

How do I identify a mushroom?

Tap «Identify», take a clear photo of the mushroom (cap, gills or pores, stem and base) or choose one from your gallery. The app gives the probable species, its confidence, whether it is edible and a safety warning. Always confirm with an expert.

Why does it ask for my location?

Only to record where the find is when you publish it. If you do not give permission, you can still publish without location, after confirming. The location becomes public together with the photo.

Do I need an account?

To identify, publish, report or block, yes. The account has no email or a sign-in key: it is anonymous and only has a display name that you choose. If you delete the app, you start from scratch. You can read the wall without an account.

How do I delete a post?

Open its page and delete it. It is removed together with its photo.

How do I delete my account?

In Settings → Delete my account. Your profile, posts, photos, reports and blocks are deleted. This cannot be undone.

How do I remove the ads?

With the Pro subscription, from the Pro screen. The ads disappear as soon as the purchase is active.

I bought Pro and it does not show

Go to Pro screen → Restore purchases. The store sometimes takes a few minutes to confirm the payment. If it still does not appear, write to us with your Apple receipt.

How do I cancel Pro?

It is managed from your Apple account: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Níscalo Pro.

What if a post is inappropriate?

Tap «Report» on its page. When it collects 3 reports it is hidden automatically and the developer reviews it. You can also block its author so you no longer see their posts.
