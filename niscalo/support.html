Níscalo · Privacy policy

Safety first: Níscalo is a helper, not an expert. Do not eat any mushroom if you have the slightest doubt, even if the app says it is edible. Always check with an expert mycologist before consuming any specimen.

Níscalo identifies mushrooms from a photo using artificial intelligence, lets you save your spots on a 3D satellite map with contour lines, elevation, slope and sun or shade, and publish your finds with a photo, a description and a location, choosing who sees the place. Something not working, or have an idea? Write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

How do I identify a mushroom?

Tap "Identify", take a clear photo of the mushroom (cap, gills or pores, stem and base) or pick one from your library. The app gives you the probable species, with its confidence, the species it can be confused with, whether it is edible and a safety warning. Always confirm with an expert. There is a limit of 40 identifications per day per account, and an internet connection is required.

What is the video I see before an analysis?

The free version shows a short full-screen video before each photo analysis, and a banner. With Níscalo Pro both go away. If no video is available (for example without a connection), the photo is not analysed: you can try again or go Pro.

Do I have to create an account?

To identify, save spots, publish, add friends, report or block, yes. The account does not ask for an email or a password: it is anonymous and only has a display name you choose. If you delete the app, you start from scratch. You can see the map and the wall without an account.

How do I save a spot?

On the Map tab, open the round button at the bottom right and tap "Save here" to save where you are using GPS, or "Add a spot by hand" and tap the map. You can also tick "Save this place to My spots" when you publish a find. Give it a name and, if you like, a note.

Are my spots private? How do I share them?

Yes: a spot is yours alone until you decide to share it. On its page, "Share" lets you choose between "With a friend", "On my wall" (anyone can see it) and "By WhatsApp or another app". You can stop sharing it whenever you like.

How do I add friends?

On the Profile tab you have your 6-character friend code. Give it to anyone you like: they type it into "Add by code" and you get the request. You are friends once you accept it.

Why does it ask for my location?

Only when you use it: to save a spot with GPS, to publish a find with its location, to center the map on your position and for the "Go" screen. If you do not grant permission, you can mark the place by hand on the map or publish without a location, after confirming. For each find you choose who sees the place: only you (the default), your friends or everyone.

What layers does the map have?

The background is Apple's satellite imagery, in 3D. On top you switch five layers on or off: Towns and roads, Trails and tracks, Streams and springs, Contour lines and Pine forests. With contour lines on, point the crosshair and it gives you the elevation, the slope and whether the point faces the sun or lies in shade, using NASA SRTM elevation data. All the controls, including the 3D button, are in the round button at the bottom right. Finds have their own tab, next to the Map. The data is approximate: do not use it as your only guide in the mountains.

What is the Pine forests layer?

It shades conifer woodland in Europe in orange, which is where the saffron milk cap grows, and it is on from the start. It uses Copernicus data (2018): it marks conifers, not mushrooms, and has no data outside Europe. It is a clue for where to look, not a guarantee that there are mushrooms.

What happens if I touch and hold the map?

The card of that point opens, with "Save spot here" and "Directions" (which opens your maps app). The app also reopens on the last tab you used.

How does the "Saffron milk caps may be out tomorrow" alert work?

It is optional and only on iPhone and iPad. Switch it on in Settings → "Alert me when mushrooms are out"; Níscalo asks for notification permission at that moment and needs a name on your profile and a saved spot or your location to know your area (approximate, about 10 km). Once a day our server checks the weather of that area with Apple WeatherKit and, if recent rain and temperatures line up, alerts you the day before, at most once every 3 days. It is an estimate: no alert with frost or heavy rain due the next day, and you may receive none. To stop it, switch it off in the same place.

How do I get to a spot?

Open the spot or the find and tap "Go": you will see the map with a line to the destination and, below it, the compass with the bearing and the distance. Tap "Turn on compass" if needed. "Directions by car" opens your maps app.

Does it work on a Mac?

Yes, on Macs with Apple silicon, as an iPhone and iPad app. The map, spots, friends and finds work the same. A Mac has no compass, so the "Go" screen gives you the bearing and the distance, and if your Mac has no camera you can identify from a photo in your library.

How do I delete a post or a spot?

Open its page and delete it. A find is removed together with its photo and its location.

How do I delete my account?

In Settings → Delete my account. Your profile, finds with their photos and locations, spots, friendships, reports and blocks are deleted. It cannot be undone.

How do I remove the ads?

With the Pro subscription, from the Pro screen. The banner and the video go away as soon as the purchase is active.

I bought Pro and it does not show up

Go to the Pro → Restore purchases screen. Sometimes the store takes a few minutes to confirm the payment. If it still does not appear, write to us with the Apple receipt.

How do I cancel the Pro subscription?

It is managed from your Apple account: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Níscalo Pro.

What if a post or a spot is inappropriate?

Tap "Report" on its page. When it collects 3 reports it is hidden automatically and the developer reviews it. You can also block its author so you do not see their posts.
