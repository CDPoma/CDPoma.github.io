In short: the app talks directly to your solar inverter over Bluetooth. There is no account or sign-up, and no personal data is collected. Your system's data stays on your phone; it only leaves it if you turn on station mode to watch it remotely, and then it travels under a random code, without your name or email.

What the app does

Data King Flow Monitor connects over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to solar inverters that speak the Voltronic/PI30 protocol and shows what your system is doing right now: what the panels are producing, whether the battery is charging or discharging, what comes in from the grid or the generator, and what the home is using.

Compatible inverters: those that speak the Voltronic/PI30 protocol over Bluetooth, either with built-in Bluetooth (such as the Axpert King or the Inverter King) or with a serial Bluetooth adapter (HM-10, JDY and similar): Axpert, Inverter King, MPP Solar, EASUN, PowMr, Must, Effekta and other compatible models. The app is compatible with that equipment; it is not an official app of any of those brands and is not affiliated with them.

Free

  • Solar generation: live watts, panel voltage and current, when the sun started and stopped, and today's sun hours. The bar splits what comes in between the home and the battery.
  • Home consumption: watts, inverter load, output voltage and frequency, and inverter temperature.
  • Battery pack: percentage, voltage, charge or discharge current and power, today's peaks and the amp-hours left. The app works out the percentage by counting the amp-hours going in and out; it is not the inverter's figure, which comes from a voltage formula.

FAQ

How do I connect?

Tap Connect and pick your inverter from the list of Bluetooth devices. Opening the app doesn't connect anything by itself; once connected, if the connection drops, the app reconnects on its own until you tap Disconnect. If your phone asks for a PIN, it's usually 123456 or 000000; if neither works, check the inverter's manual.

Why doesn't the percentage match the inverter's?

The inverter's figure comes from a voltage formula and is far off with lithium batteries (it can show 0% with the battery half full). The app counts the amp-hours going in and out and learns your battery's curve. For it to be right, enter your bank's real capacity in Settings: the sum of the amp-hours of the batteries connected in parallel.

How does station mode work?

On the phone that stays next to the inverter (for example an old one, plugged in), connect to the inverter and tap the antenna icon → Turn on station mode. On your phone, tap the satellite icon and enter the 6-character code. While station mode is on, that phone's screen stays on. From the remote view, after refueling the generator, the app asks how many liters are in the tank.

Why doesn't the screen turn off?

On purpose: while the app is open it keeps the screen on, so a phone working as a station doesn't fall asleep and stop reading the inverter. When you close the app, the phone turns the screen off as usual.

Can I see a card bigger?

Yes: double-tap a card and it goes full screen; another double tap puts it back.

I paid for Pro and it doesn't show

Open the Pro screen (the Pro button) and tap Restore purchases, with the same store account you paid with.

Privacy policy

What is stored on your phone

The inverter readings are stored only on your device: one point per minute for today and one summary per day (up to 400 days) for each measurement, for the charts. The app also stores the settings you enter (bank capacity, generator details, language), the learned curve of your battery, the Bluetooth identifier of the last inverter (to reconnect without picking it again) and, if you use station mode or remote view, its code. Everything is deleted when you uninstall the app.

What leaves your phone, and only if you use station mode or remote view

With station mode on, the station phone publishes to Google Firebase (Realtime Database, on servers in the European Union, and Cloud Firestore) under its random 6-character code:

  • the current inverter reading (watts, volts, amps, percentages and the app's calculations, such as the runtime hours);
  • today's history and the daily summary for the last 120 days, the generator's hours, the estimated diesel, the cycles and the measured battery capacity;
  • a technical status of the station (whether it is publishing and the last error), so it can be diagnosed if it goes silent.

The phone watching remotely writes, under that same code, an "I'm watching" signal (the time, whether the app is in front and a random session number), so the station only publishes when someone is watching, and the "tank refilled" command with the liters when you use it.

None of this includes your name, email, account, location or device identifiers: the app has none of that data. What is published is replaced with each update and stays under the code until the next one; if you want us to delete it, email us the code and we will.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth: to find the inverter and read it.
  • Location (Android before version 12 only): the system requires it to scan for Bluetooth devices; the app doesn't read or use your location.
  • Tracking (iPhone and iPad): Apple's prompt to personalize ads. Saying no doesn't remove any feature.
  • Wi-Fi state (Android): only used by an installation set up by hand as the device owner, to restart the Wi-Fi of a station that loses its connection. On a normal phone it does nothing.

The app doesn't ask for camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

Children

This is a technical tool for solar installations, not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect data from children.
