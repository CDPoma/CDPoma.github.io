Prompt AI

Support and privacy

In short: there are no accounts. No sign-up, no email, no password. Your projects, notes and prompts are stored on your device. Something only leaves it in seven cases: when the AI generates a prompt or organizes a note (with Prompt AI's AI or, if you add your own key, directly with your provider), when you share a project or a link, when you report content, in the free version what the ads need, the check for a new version of the app, on Android an encrypted (hashed) phone identifier so the 7-day free trial can't be repeated, and some minimal, anonymous usage statistics, explained below. You decide whether ads can be personalized, and the one-time “No Banner” purchase removes the banner, the app-open ad and the rewarded ads. The count of today's prompts is stored only on your phone.

Something not working, or have a question? Email gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

How does it work?

Create a project and, inside it, type or dictate your idea in the Prompts tab, pick App, Web or Game next to “Save” and save. The AI turns it into a step-by-step work brief. The original idea is kept with the prompt: tap “Idea” to flip the card, edit it and regenerate the prompt. In the Notes tab the AI only organizes what you write, without adding or changing anything.

What language does the AI write in?

The language you write your idea in. The app's menus and messages are in English, Spanish (Spain) and Latin American Spanish: the first time it uses your device's language, and you can change it in Settings (the gear, top right) → Language.

The AI doesn't respond or says it's overloaded

Generating takes from a few seconds to a minute, and the app shows how long it has been. If the AI doesn't answer, the app tells you instead of saving a half-finished prompt: you can try again in a minute, or keep your text as it is with “Save without AI”. When you regenerate and it fails, the prompt you already had is left untouched.

How many prompts can I generate for free?

One free prompt a day (your phone's day). From the second one of the day, each new or regenerated prompt comes after a rewarded ad that you choose to watch (“Watch an ad to generate”): it has to be watched to the end and, if you close it early, nothing is generated; if no ad is available at that moment, the prompt is generated anyway. Saving a note never counts. With Pro or during the free trial, no limit and no ad.

How do I share a project?

Open the project → the “…” button at the top (or touch and hold its icon on the home screen) → Share → Turn into a shared project. In the free version you share after a rewarded ad (“Watch an ad to share”, once per project); with Pro, with no ad. You get a six-character code; send it to anyone you like, by WhatsApp or by showing it to them. They enter it in Settings → Join a shared project, free after a rewarded ad each time they join (“Watch an ad to join”) or with no ad with Pro. Sharing a single note or prompt by link is free, with no ad.

What does Pro remove?

The one-time “No Banner” purchase removes the banner, the app-open ad and every rewarded ad (unlimited prompts, and sharing or joining with no ad). Linking notes with prompts is free for everyone: Pro isn't needed. The full-screen ad shown while the AI works stays with Pro too, and the purchase screen says so before you pay.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

In Settings → Go Pro → Try 7 days free. It gives everything in Pro for 7 days, once, and it never charges or renews: when it ends, the free version returns. It isn't repeated if you delete and reinstall the app: on iPhone it is a zero-price App Store purchase, which comes back with Restore purchases with its original start date; on Android, the encrypted phone record explained below. On Android, starting it needs a connection.

The app asks me to update

When a newer version than yours has been published, the app shows “Update the app” with an “Update now” button that opens the store. A connection error never blocks it.

I got a new phone and I don't have Pro

Go to Settings → Go Pro → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple or Google Play account you paid with. Anyone who bought the one-time purchase from the first versions keeps Pro, and anyone who had the subscription keeps Pro while it stays active, with nothing to do.

I have the old subscription: how do I cancel it?

The monthly and yearly subscription is no longer sold: Pro is now the one-time “No Banner” purchase, with no renewals and nothing to cancel. If you were already subscribed, you stay Pro while the subscription is active. To cancel it, go to your store account, not the app. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

What data leaves your device, and why

  • An anonymous identifier. When you open the app online, Firebase Authentication (Google) gives your device a random identifier. It has no name, email, phone number or password, and you don't see any form. It's used so that only the members of a shared project can read it, and so that someone who writes something inappropriate can be reported or removed.
  • Your alias, if you set one. It's the name others see in shared projects. It's optional and stored in Firebase. If you don't set one, you appear as “Anonymous”.
  • The projects you share (free after watching a rewarded ad, or with no ad with Pro). When you share a project, its name, notes and prompts are stored in Cloud Firestore (Google), along with the alias and anonymous identifier of whoever wrote each item. That's how it syncs between its members. Only people who joined with its six-character code can read it. The rest of your projects stay on your device.
  • The notes or prompts you share by link. A read-only copy with the title and text is stored so that whoever receives the link can open it. Anyone with the link can see it.
  • The version check. When you open the app, when you return to it and at most once a day, the app asks which is the latest published version: on iPhone, Apple (App Store, itunes.apple.com, giving the app's identifier, not yours) and our Firebase Hosting site (prompt-ai-legal.web.app, a file with the minimum version), and on Android only that site. Only the normal network request is sent (at least your IP address), with no identifier of yours and nothing from your projects. If your version is too old, the app asks you to update it from the store.
  • On Android, an encrypted device identifier, only for the free trial. So that the 7-day trial can't be repeated by deleting and reinstalling the app, the app computes an encrypted digest (SHA-256 hash) of your phone's Android identifier for this app (ANDROID_ID). The identifier itself never leaves the phone and is never stored: only that digest, from which the identifier cannot be recovered. With it the app asks Cloud Firestore (Google) whether this phone has already started the trial and, if you start it, a record with that digest and the server's start date and time is saved. It contains no name, email, advertising ID, the anonymous identifier above or anything from your projects, and it is not used for anything else: neither ads nor statistics. It isn't used on iPhone, where the App Store handles the trial.
  • What stays only on your phone. The count of today's prompts (how many free prompts you've used today and the rewarded ads you've watched and not yet used) is stored only in the app's storage on your device: it isn't sent to any server.
  • Reports. When you report content, it stops showing on your device and a record of the report is kept: a copy of that content as it was, the reason, and the anonymous identifiers and aliases of who wrote it and who reported it. Nobody reviews reports one by one; the record is used to handle a complaint if one arrives.

The AI: generating prompts and organizing notes

When you write an idea and tap to generate a prompt, or save a note in the Notes tab (the AI only organizes it, without adding or changing anything), that text is sent to a server function that we run, hosted on Supabase. That function passes it to Claude, Anthropic's AI, and returns the result to you.

It goes through there, rather than straight to Anthropic, so that the AI key doesn't have to ship inside the app. We don't store your text: not on that server and not in any database. It's discarded as soon as the result is returned.

Anthropic handles that data under its commercial terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy), so don't write confidential information or other people's personal data there. If you don't use that feature, nothing is sent.

With your own AI (optional)

In Settings → Your own AI (optional) you can add the API key from your own AI account: Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI), Gemini (Google) or an OpenAI-compatible service you choose (for example DeepSeek, Mistral, xAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Azure OpenAI, or Ollama and LM Studio on a computer on your own network; only then will the iPhone ask for local network access). It's optional; without a key everything works as described above.

  • What is sent, and to whom. With your key, the text of your idea or note and the app's instructions are sent directly from your phone to the provider you chose, with your key. They don't go through our Supabase server and our key isn't used. Before the first send to each provider the app shows a notice saying what is sent and to whom, and sends nothing unless you accept.
  • Your key. It's stored encrypted in your device's keychain (Keychain on iPhone; storage encrypted with the Android Keystore on Android), isn't synced to iCloud or moved to another device when restoring a backup, and is only sent to the provider you chose. We never see or receive it, and it doesn't appear in logs or statistics.
  • What the provider does. It handles the text under its own terms and privacy policy, and the usage is billed to your account with it. For example: Anthropic (anthropic.com/legal/privacy), OpenAI (openai.com/policies/privacy-policy) and Google (policies.google.com/privacy).
  • To remove it, Settings → Your own AI → Delete my key: it's deleted from the device and the app goes back to Prompt AI's AI. On Android it's also deleted when you uninstall the app; on iPhone the keychain may keep it after uninstalling, and it's deleted with that same button.
  • The free-tier limits (1 prompt a day and a rewarded ad for each extra one) are the same with or without your key.

Ads

The app shows Google AdMob ads: a banner, a full-screen ad while the AI works (when generating a prompt or organizing a note), announced on screen beforehand, from time to time one when you open the app (1 in every 5 openings, never the first one nor on the day you install the app) and, only if you ask for it, a rewarded ad: to generate a prompt once you've used the day's free prompt (each new or regenerated prompt from the second one of the day), to share a project (once per project, when you turn it into a shared one) or to join one with its code (every time you join). It has to be watched to the end, and if you close it early nothing is generated, shared or joined; if no ad is available at that moment, it's done anyway. Before any ad is shown:

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google shows you its consent form.
  • On iPhone and iPad, Apple asks whether you allow the app to track your activity.

If you allow it, Google can use your device's advertising identifier to personalize and measure ads. If you don't, ads still appear, but they aren't personalized. On Android outside Europe you aren't asked: Google applies your device's ad settings. In every case AdMob receives technical data from the device, such as the IP address, model or operating system. It uses it to show the ad, limit how often it appears and prevent fraud. How Google uses it: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can change your mind at any time:

  • In Europe, from the app: Settings (the gear, top right) → Ad privacy options.
  • On iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can delete the advertising ID.

With the “No Banner” purchase, or during the 7-day free trial, the banner, the app-open ad and the rewarded ads go away (unlimited prompts, and sharing a project or joining one with no ad). Only the full-screen ad shown while the AI works stays, also with Pro.

Usage statistics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase only to know how many people use it and which screens are used. It records only what Firebase measures on its own: that the app opens, when a session starts and ends, which screens are viewed and how long it is active. It also receives technical data from the device (model, system version, language and approximate country) and a random installation identifier, which changes if you reinstall the app.

It doesn't use the advertising identifier, doesn't receive your name, email or any user identifier, doesn't see the content of your projects, notes or prompts, and isn't used to personalize ads. That installation identifier is also shared with RevenueCat so that purchases show up in the same statistics.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland it's only turned on if you allow it in Google's consent form, and you can change that at any time in Ad privacy options (Settings, the gear, top right). Outside that area the law doesn't require asking and it's turned on without a form. Google Analytics keeps that data for a limited time, at most 14 months. When you uninstall the app, everything stops being sent.

The Pro purchase

Prompt AI Pro is the one-time “No Banner” purchase (€1.99 in Spain; in other countries the app shows the price in your currency before you pay): a single payment, with no subscription and no renewals, yours forever. It removes the banner and the app-open ad, removes the rewarded ads that the free version shows (unlimited prompts a day, and sharing projects and joining one with its code with no ad); the full-screen ad shown while the AI works stays. Linking notes with prompts is free for everyone: Pro isn't needed. Until October 22, 2026 Pro was a subscription of €1.99/month or €14.99/year; it is no longer offered, and anyone who has it stays Pro while it is active (it's cancelled from your Apple or Google Play account). Payment is processed by Apple or Google Play. RevenueCat validates it: the app asks it for the status of your purchase when it opens (also if you haven't bought anything), and it receives a random app identifier and the purchase details. It doesn't receive your name or the content of your projects. “Restore purchases” is on the same screen, to get Pro back when you change devices.

The 7-day free trial

The trial gives the same as Pro for 7 days, once, and it charges nothing and doesn't renew when it ends: the free version returns. On iPhone it is a zero-price App Store purchase (“7-day Trial”): Apple processes it, the App Store sets its start date and RevenueCat sees it like any other purchase. On Android, since Google Play doesn't allow zero-price purchases, the trial is tied to the phone: a record with the encrypted digest (hash) of the phone identifier and the start date set by the server is stored in Cloud Firestore, as explained above in “What data leaves your device”. That record is only used so the trial isn't repeated: the app can't change or delete it, and that's why “Delete my shared data” doesn't delete it either. Starting it needs a connection.

What the app does NOT do

  • We don't record the content of your projects, notes or prompts, or what you type: the usage statistics only count that the app opens and which screens are used.
  • It has no tracking of its own: the only tracking is Google's, for ads, explained above. The usage statistics don't use the advertising identifier.
  • We don't sell or hand over your data to anyone.
  • It doesn't ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location. If you dictate, your device's keyboard does it.

Deleting your data

There's no account to delete. To remove what you've shared, in the app go to Settings (the gear, top right) → Delete my shared data:

  • Shared projects you created are deleted with all their content and their code.
  • You leave other people's projects, and what you wrote in them is deleted; they keep the rest.
  • Your alias is deleted and your device starts over with a new identifier.

Anything that's only on your device isn't touched, and it disappears when you uninstall the app.

The Android free-trial record (the encrypted digest of the phone identifier and its start date) isn't deleted from the app, because its only purpose is that the trial isn't repeated. It contains nothing that identifies you by itself; if you still want us to delete it, email us, knowing that the app will then be unable to tell that this phone already used it.

Copies shared by link and reports can't be deleted from the app. Email us at the address below with the link or whatever you want removed, and we'll delete it.

How long data is kept

  • Shared projects: until they're deleted. Each one keeps its 300 most recent prompts; older ones are deleted automatically. Notes are never deleted automatically.
  • Link copies: until you ask us to remove them.
  • Android free-trial record: for as long as the app exists, because it's what keeps the trial from being repeated on that phone.
  • Reports: until you ask us to delete them, or for as long as they're needed to handle a complaint about that content.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 14 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.

Legal basis and your rights

We process data for three reasons. First, to provide the service you ask for: generating prompts, organizing notes, sharing and the purchase. Second, your consent, for personalized ads and, where the law requires it, for the usage statistics. Third, our legitimate interest in moderating shared content and preventing abuse, such as repeating the 7-day free trial on the same phone (the Android record above).

You can access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its use, or ask for a copy. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. For any of these, email us. You can also file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Who it's shared with

Only with the services that make the app work:

  • Google: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Hosting (the minimum-version file), and AdMob.
  • Anthropic: the AI (Claude) that generates the prompts and organizes the notes.
  • If you add your own AI key: the provider you choose (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or another), only with the text you send to generate or organize.
  • Supabase: the AI's intermediate server.
  • RevenueCat: validation of the purchase and of the iPhone trial (the Android one is kept by Cloud Firestore).
  • Apple and Google Play: payment; Apple, also, the check of the latest published version.

Some of them process data outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards required by the GDPR: standard contractual clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
