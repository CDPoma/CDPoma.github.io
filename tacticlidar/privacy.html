Privacy policy

Help and support

Summary: TacticLiDAR measures distances with the LiDAR sensor of an iPhone or iPad Pro and shows them in colour, and it processes everything on the device itself. There is no account, no sign-up and no server of our own. The live depth image is never saved or sent anywhere. If you record a video, it stays on the device (inside the app) and only leaves it if you save it to Photos or share it. The app shows Google ads (a banner, an app-open ad and a rewarded ad), which are only personalised if you give your consent. A subscription, TacticLiDAR Pro, removes the banner and the app-open ad and lets you record with no time limit. To know how many people use the app we also use anonymous usage analytics from Google Analytics for Firebase, with no advertising identifier and with your consent where the law requires it.

Who is responsible

Gabriel Micó Victoria, developer of TacticLiDAR. For any question about data, or to exercise your rights, write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Camera and LiDAR sensor

The app asks for permission to use the camera: the LiDAR sensor of the iPhone or iPad Pro is part of the camera, and without that permission it cannot measure. LiDAR emits its own infrared light and measures the distance to whatever is in front of it; the app turns that into a colour image. The live depth image is never saved or sent.

If you turn on “Amplified camera”, the app also uses the camera image, pushed to its maximum, blended with the depth image to see further where there is some light. That image is processed on the device only and does not leave it. Where there is light, that image can show a face or a piece of text; if you record a video with the feature on, that ends up in the video, which stays on your device (see below). “Far points” and “Walking map” (“Mapa al caminar”) only do calculations with the same measurements, on the device and in memory while the app is open: no map is saved or sent.

Photos: add only

When you choose to save a video to Photos, the app asks for add-only access to Photos. With that permission the app can put the video in your library, but it cannot see or read your photos or videos. If you deny it, you can keep using the app and your recordings stay in it.

What is stored and where

  • On the device: the recordings you make (each video with its thumbnail and a small file with the date and length), in the app's folder. They are not included in the iCloud backup. Also stored, on the device only: your settings (chosen environment, range, palette, frame rate and the other panel values), the counter for the app-open ad and the date of the first launch, and the last subscription status confirmed by Apple (plan and renewal date), so the app keeps working offline.
  • On no server of ours: TacticLiDAR has no server and no user account. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts or the photos in your library.
  • What you take out of the device: when you save a video to Photos, it sits in your photo library under whatever iCloud Photos settings you have. When you share it, the app opens the device's share sheet and you choose which app or person gets the file. A video can only be shared once it has been saved to Photos.

Apart from Google's ads and the usage analytics described below, we use no other analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell or hand data to anyone.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, in three formats:

  • Banner at the bottom of the main screen. Free version only.
  • App-open ad, full screen, one in every five times the app is opened (on launch, or on returning after 30 minutes or more away), never on the day of installation. Free version only.
  • Rewarded ad, full screen and video, which you choose to watch: (1) in the free version, to keep recording when the 10 free seconds run out; (2) to save a video to Photos, in the free version and with Pro alike. If no ad is available, or you have not given consent for ads, it is not shown and the action is granted anyway. If you close it before it ends, it is not granted.

Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, before requesting any ad the app shows Google's consent form (UMP), and then Apple's tracking permission (ATT). If you consent, Google may show personalised ads and use the device's advertising identifier (IDFA), along with technical data such as the IP address and the device model, under Google's policy. If you do not, ads still appear, but not personalised. Google receives nothing of what the sensor measures or of your videos. People who pay for Pro also see this form, because the rewarded ad for saving a video stays.

You can change your choice at any time in the device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and, where Google's form is required, from the “Ad privacy” option on the TacticLiDAR Pro screen.

Usage analytics

TacticLiDAR uses Google Analytics for Firebase to know how many people use the app and when, so we can improve it.

  • What is collected: app usage data, that is, automatic events for opening, session and screens viewed (first open, session start, time of use, screen shown); an identifier of the app installation on your device, which is not your name, nor your email, nor the advertising identifier; and basic technical data: approximate country, operating system, app version and device model.
  • What is not collected: we do not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA) with Analytics, we assign no user identifier or personal properties, and nothing of what the sensor measures, of your recordings or of your photos is sent. Analytics does not ask for Apple's tracking permission.
  • What for: only for usage statistics (active users, versions and countries of use). This data is not linked to your name, your email or any account (TacticLiDAR has no accounts), and it is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites, nor for advertising. For Apple, the installation identifier counts as a device identifier, and that is why it appears that way in the App Store privacy label.
  • With whom: Google processes it as a data processor.

Consent and control. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics stays off until you answer Google's consent form shown when the app opens (the same one as for ads): it only turns on if you accept storing data on your device, and if you refuse, no usage data is collected. Outside that area, where prior consent is not required, analytics is on by default; if the app cannot tell which country you are in, it stays off. You can ask for it to stop being used by writing to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com; you can also delete the app and install it again, and the form will show again in the European area.

TacticLiDAR Pro subscription

TacticLiDAR Pro is an auto-renewing subscription: €1.99 a month or €14.99 a year (in Spain; in other countries the app shows the local price before you pay). It removes the banner and the app-open ad and lets you record with no time limit; it does not remove the rewarded ad for saving a video to Photos. If your Apple ID has not yet used the trial, the first period includes 7 free days: before you pay the app shows the date of the first charge, and you can cancel without paying at least 24 hours before. Apple processes the payment with your Apple ID; we never see payment details. The app checks the subscription directly with the App Store (StoreKit), with no third-party services and sending nothing to any server of ours. To cancel: device Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or the “Manage subscription” button on the Pro screen. If you change device, “Restore purchases” is on that same screen.

Version check

When the app opens and when you return to it, TacticLiDAR asks Apple (itunes.apple.com/lookup) which version is on sale in the App Store. If it is newer than the installed one, the app asks you to update to keep using it, with a button that opens its App Store page. The request only carries the app identifier (com.tacticlidar.app), the country (es) and a timestamp; it sends no personal data or anything the sensor measures, although Apple, like any server, sees the IP address it connects from. Offline or on any error, the app never blocks.

Deleting your data

Since everything is on the device, you are in control: in the recordings list, delete a video (it asks you to confirm) and it is removed along with its thumbnail. If you delete the app, the device deletes all the recordings with it. Videos you already saved to Photos are deleted from the Photos app, and those you shared with other people or apps are already in their hands. We hold no copy of anything to delete on our side. The subscription is not deleted with the app: you cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Children and rights

TacticLiDAR asks for no personal data. You can exercise your GDPR rights (access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability) by writing to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com, and lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Legal basis: personalised ads, on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time; non-personalised ads, on legitimate interest in funding the free version; usage analytics, on your consent in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (which you can withdraw at any time) and, outside that area, on legitimate interest in improving the app.
