Help and support

Privacy policy

TacticLiDAR uses the LiDAR sensor of the iPhone or iPad Pro, which emits its own infrared light and measures distances, to show you a live colour depth image: it also works in total darkness, with a range of about 5 metres (16 ft). It is not “real” night vision or a thermal camera, and it is not a photo. Everything is processed on the device itself and with no account. Something not working? Write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

What does it do, exactly?

It uses LiDAR to measure the distance of everything in front of you and paints it by colour (near, far), with the distance in metres at the centre of the screen (“the crosshair”). It helps you make out the shapes of people, furniture, doors and steps where there is no light. You can record video of what you see on screen (the coloured image with the crosshair and the scale; never the banner or the panel).

Which devices do I need?

One with a LiDAR sensor: iPhone 12 Pro, 12 Pro Max or any later Pro or Pro Max model, and iPad Pro with LiDAR (2020 onwards). iOS 17 or later is required. On a device without LiDAR (iPhones without “Pro”, regular iPads) the app explains it on screen and cannot measure.

What are its limits? (we say it plainly)

  • Range of about 5 metres. Beyond that, the device's LiDAR does not measure reliably.
  • Low resolution (256×192 points): you see shapes and distances, not detail. No real colours, no faces, no text, except with “Amplified camera” (below).
  • The sun blinds LiDAR. Sunlight carries a lot of infrared and, outdoors by day, valid points drop. It works best at night, in the shade or indoors.
  • Very shiny, transparent (glass) or very black surfaces are measured poorly.
  • The device may warm up with long use, more so when recording. If the temperature climbs a lot the app warns you, and at critical level it stops the sensor and you can resume once it cools down.

What are “Narrow indoor space”, “Large indoor space”, “Outdoors” and “Automatic”?

They are the environments: each one adjusts range, colour palette, frame rate and smoothing. Narrow indoor space (corridors, small rooms: short range, more frames per second), Large indoor space (living rooms, warehouses), Outdoors (the sun limits it a lot; raises the range and smooths) and Automatic, which picks one from the distances it sees, and the panel says which one and why. You can tweak each environment's values in the panel and go back to the factory ones whenever you like.

What are “Far points”, “Amplified camera” and “Walking map”?

Three free-for-everyone features, with their limits. “Far points” and “Amplified camera” can be combined; the map is a separate view:

  • Far points: shows, dimmed, the unreliable points beyond about 5 m. It can gain one or two metres, with more noise.
  • Amplified camera: if there is some light (moon, street lamps, a city in the distance), the camera pushed to maximum is blended with the LiDAR to see further. In total darkness it adds nothing (the app warns: “No light for the camera: LiDAR only”). Where there is light, a face or a piece of text may be visible, and if you record it ends up in the video, on your device.
  • Walking map: in the “View” selector on the main screen (next to the environment one), choose “Map”. The 3D mesh of what you have already seen stays painted, coloured by distance, as you move. It remembers what you saw; it does not see further. It uses ARKit, which tracks your position with the camera and the motion sensors, so in “Map” “Far points”, “Amplified camera”, smoothing and frame rate do not apply. In total darkness, tracking may drift and the map may twist or stop growing (the app warns you). The map is lost when you leave the app.
  • Stealth mode: a button on the main screen, free. It sets the brightness to minimum and switches to the Night green palette so you are not dazzled in the dark; on exit it gives your brightness back. It can be turned on and off at any time with the viewer open, also while recording (the video turns green from that moment, because it records what you see). Ads show before or after, at normal brightness.
  • Goggles mode: the "Goggles" button on the main screen, free. It splits the screen in two, in landscape, for a Cardboard-type phone VR viewer: it is the same depth map in each eye, not stereoscopic 3D. No banner or ads while you wear it. Tap anywhere on the screen to exit. If you record, each eye shows "REC". Not available on an iPad in a window.

What is “No Banner” and how much does it cost? And the 7-day trial?

“No Banner” is a one-time purchase of €1.99 (in other countries the app shows the local price before you pay): you pay once and it does not renew. It removes the banner, the app-open ad and the rewarded-ad step before recording; it does not remove the ad shown when you finish recording. It is on the “No Banner” button on the main screen. The 7-day trial (“Try 7 days free”, on that same screen) is free, can be used only once per Apple ID and gives the same as “No Banner” for 7 days; when it ends, the free version's ads come back. Reinstalling the app or restoring purchases does not give another 7 days. The new features (Far points, Amplified camera and Walking map) are free, with or without “No Banner”.

How do I ask for a refund?

“No Banner” is a one-time payment and the 7-day trial charges nothing: there are no recurring charges. For a refund of the purchase, Apple handles it at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought “No Banner” and still see the banner

Open the “No Banner” screen (the “No Banner” button on the main screen) and tap “Restore Purchases”, with the same Apple ID you bought with. It also works when you change device or reinstall the app. Remember that with “No Banner” the ad shown when you finish recording stays.

Where are my recordings stored? Do they leave the device?

They stay on the device, in the app's recordings list (they do not go to the iCloud backup). They only leave if you save them to Photos or share them. The live depth image is never saved or sent. To delete one, do it from the list (it asks you to confirm); if you already saved it to Photos, delete it there as well. More details in the privacy policy.

The app asks for camera and Photos permission

The camera is needed because LiDAR is part of it. The Photos permission is add-only: it is used to save the video you choose, and the app cannot see or read your photos. If you denied it, turn it on in Settings → TacticLiDAR → Photos.

Why does it ask me to update the app?

If a newer version is on the App Store, the app asks you to update to keep using it; your recordings stay on the device and you will see them after updating. To know this it asks Apple for the version on sale (itunes.apple.com/lookup, no personal data). Offline or on any error, the app never blocks.

The image is rotated, empty or very noisy

Try another environment (by hand, rather than Automatic), get closer to what you are looking at (the range is about 5 m) and avoid direct sun and glass surfaces. If the sensor stays paused or without frames, the on-screen notice offers to try another depth source. If it is still wrong, write to us with the device model.
