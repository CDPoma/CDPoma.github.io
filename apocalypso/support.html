ApocalypSO · Privacy policy

ApocalypSO is an app to talk, call for help and find your way without the internet: nearby phones form a mesh over Bluetooth, direct wifi or LoRa radio, and the app includes a survival library, maps and tools that work offline. Something not working? Write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

How do I talk to someone without internet?

Install the app on both phones and stay close: the mesh forms by itself over Bluetooth and direct wifi. Nearby lists the people in range and in Chats you can write to the general channel or privately. To add someone as a contact, scan each other's QR in Contacts. Private and group messages are encrypted; the general channel can be read by anyone in the mesh. Messages hop from phone to phone, so they travel further the more devices there are in between.

Why does it ask for Bluetooth and location?

Bluetooth (and the local network) is used to find nearby devices and form the mesh; without that permission there is no mesh. Location (only while the app is in use) is used for the map, GPS, compass and SOS. Your position only leaves the phone when you send an SOS or choose to share it. The camera is only used to read QR codes, and the flashlight for Morse code. More details in the privacy policy.

How do I send an SOS and how do I search for someone else's?

To call for help, press and hold the SOS button (top right) for 2 seconds and confirm. The SOS is relayed across the whole mesh, with your position if GPS has one. To search for someone else's SOS, tap Search on the alert or in Nearby → Recent SOS. The guide shows an arrow towards the person with the distance and the GPS accuracy; if you are already very close, it asks you to look around, because at that range GPS gives no reliable direction. If the SOS carries no position, it helps with "cold / warm" based on signal strength. Search only if it is safe and, if you can, also call the emergency services.

What is Pro and how do I restore my purchases?

The essentials are free: the built-in articles, the world map, the mesh and the tools. Pro is a one-time payment (not a subscription) that includes all the groups of the extended library and removes all ads. The groups can also be bought separately; they do not remove the ads. To recover what you bought on another device or after reinstalling, open the app's store (Extended library) and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple account. Purchases are on iPhone and iPad; Mac and Windows do not sell anything for now.

How do I pass the app to another phone without internet?

From Share: 1) "Prepare content" (only once), 2) "Start server", 3) show the QR under "How to connect". The other phone scans it and opens, in its browser, a portal with the manuals and the content. Both devices must be on the same wifi network or, if there is none, on one phone's hotspot (on iPhone, turn on "Personal Hotspot" in Settings). Note: the portal links to the App Store for anyone who does not have the app yet, and installing it from the App Store does need internet; without internet you can hand over the content and manuals, but not install the app on an iPhone. Content packs can also be passed from phone to phone over the mesh.

Is my family alerted by SMS when I send an SOS?

If you save emergency contacts in My node (up to five, on iPhone and iPad), sending an SOS opens a ready-written SMS for them with your position; you only tap "Send", because Apple does not let an app send SMS by itself. It needs mobile coverage: it is a normal SMS from your carrier. The mesh works the same with no coverage. Next to SOS there is also a button to call the emergency number of your region (112 by default), which opens the system call screen for you to confirm.

What is Ultra saver mode?

It is the battery-saving mode: dim screen, Bluetooth in bursts, GPS only when a tool needs it and a radar without animation. The app picks the mode by itself depending on whether there is internet: with internet it runs in "Maximum range" (continuous Bluetooth and normal screen) and without internet it switches to "Ultra saver", so the battery lasts much longer. To change it by hand, open the Battery panel on the home screen; your choice stays until you close the app.

What is stealth mode?

It is for anyone who does not want to be found: the phone stops announcing itself on the mesh, scanning for others and forwarding other people's packets, and the screen stays at minimum, with no sounds or flashlight. "Sync now" opens 15 seconds to receive and deliver what is pending. If you send an SOS you leave stealth so you can be helped (the app warns you first).

Can I use a LoRa radio?

Yes. If you have a Meshtastic radio, connect it from LoRa (or "My node"); the texts from that LoRa network will show in the app and yours will go out through it. It is optional: the Bluetooth and wifi mesh works without a radio.

Where are my messages stored and how do I delete them?

Only on your phone: there are no accounts and no copy on any server. Uninstalling the app deletes everything; you can also regenerate your identity from the app to start over. Messages that other devices already received stay on them.

Does the medical information replace a doctor?

No. It is for guidance only and is not a substitute for a healthcare professional or the emergency services.
