What CamPhone is

CamPhone turns one phone into a camera and another into a viewer, linked with a code. How data is handled is in the privacy policy.

Following the law

Whoever installs and uses CamPhone is responsible for following the law of their country when recording images, sound, license plates or people.

Reliability

Motion detection, people detection and license plate reading can fail (distance, light, angle, dirt, rain, connection). CamPhone is not a substitute for an alarm or a certified security system.

Vigilante Pro subscription and “No Banner” purchase

“No Banner” is a one-time purchase (no subscription).
