CamPhone: your old phone as a security camera that detects motion

One device is the camera and another is the viewer, linked with a code

What is CamPhone

CamPhone is an iPhone and iPad app that turns a device you no longer use into a security camera that detects motion, records clips and notifies the viewer, and lets you watch it live from another iPhone or iPad. The two are linked with a 10-character code, with no accounts or sign-up. Picture and sound go directly from one device to the other, encrypted, and recordings are stored on the camera.

Quick facts

  • Price: free with ads (one banner). Optional one-time “No Banner” purchase for €1.99, no subscription.
  • Devices: iPhone and iPad, in any combination of camera and viewer.
  • Network: Wi-Fi or mobile data (4G and 5G), on either device.
  • Accounts: none. They are linked with a 10-character code.
  • Cloud: none. Video goes directly between the two devices and recordings stay on the camera.
  • Alerts: system notification, optional and off by default, when the camera saves a clip.
  • Languages: Spanish and English.

How it works

  1. Choose “Camera” on the old iPhone or iPad: it shows a 10-character code. Leave it plugged in with the app open.
  2. Choose “Viewer” on your other iPhone or iPad and type the code once.
  3. Watch, look and review: the camera records a clip on its own when it detects motion, you watch it live whenever you want, you turn its surveillance on or off from the viewer and you review the recordings, which stay on the camera.

Surveillance with motion detection

With surveillance on, the camera watches all the time. If it detects motion it records a clip; when the motion stops, it stops recording and keeps watching. You can adjust the sensitivity (Low, Medium or High) on the camera.

Detection happens inside the camera device: no image is sent to a server to be analysed. It only detects motion: it can't tell people, animals or shadows apart, and it may give extra alerts or miss some movement. Motion detection and automatic recording have been tested on a real iPhone.

If you switch on “Notify me when the camera records” on the viewer (it is off by default) and allow iOS notifications, you get an alert each time the camera saves a clip, even with the viewer's app closed, at most one every 30 seconds. The alert carries no picture or video. It has been checked on a real iPhone with the viewer's app in the background; alerts depend on iOS and your connection.

Turn surveillance on and off from the viewer

From the viewer you turn surveillance off and on without touching the camera: for example, switch it off when you get home and on when you leave. The camera's app doesn't close: it stays open and connected. The viewer shows the camera's real state, not the last command sent. With surveillance off, the sensor stays closed and only opens if you watch live or tap “Record”.

Watch live whenever you want

Picture and sound go straight from the camera to the viewer over WebRTC (encrypted), with a choice of quality (Low, Medium or High). Works on Wi-Fi and on mobile data (4G and 5G): the camera only needs a phone with a SIM, no Wi-Fi. On mobile data the video uses your plan's data; the “Low” quality saves data. If your network doesn't allow a direct connection, a Cloudflare relay server forwards the already-encrypted packets without being able to see them.

Recordings stay on the camera

Motion clips and manual recordings are always saved on the camera's device, never on the viewer or in a cloud. From the viewer you open “Recordings” and play one from the camera; on the camera you can send it to the photo library or delete it. The library keeps up to 6 GB and deletes the oldest.

Baby monitor, pet cam and security camera

Use a spare phone as a baby monitor, a pet cam, a home security camera or a small-business camera: anywhere an old iPhone or iPad, plugged in, can watch while you see the picture from your own phone.

What it does and doesn't do (limits)

  • Camera: leave it plugged in with the app open. It watches, records a clip when there is motion and always shows on screen what it is doing (watching, recording or off).
  • Viewer: type the code once and watch live, with sound, pick the quality, switch between the rear and front camera, turn surveillance on or off and switch alerts on.
  • Secure code: 10 random symbols; “Change code” locks the viewer out instantly.
  • It is not a substitute for an alarm, a professional security system or supervision by a person.
  • It does not upload video to any cloud: recordings stay on the camera.
  • It does not work with the app closed on iOS: the camera only watches with CamPhone open in front.
  • Detection can fail or give extra alerts, and it detects motion, not sound or crying.
  • It only works between devices running CamPhone. One viewer at a time watches each camera live.

Requirements

  • One iPhone or iPad as the camera and another as the viewer, in any combination.
  • iOS does not allow the camera to be used with the app closed or in the background: the camera device must be plugged in, with the screen on and CamPhone open in front. With surveillance on, its camera is on (iOS shows the usage indicator); the microphone is only used while recording or watching live.
  • The camera screen never turns off: after 20 seconds without a touch it drops to a low but visible brightness, and a touch brings it back. The camera's banner always stays in view, even with “No Banner”.
  • Internet on both devices (Wi-Fi or mobile data; the camera only needs a phone with a SIM). Only one viewer can be connected to each camera.

Responsible use

Meant for watching your home, business or pets with the knowledge of anyone who appears, not for recording people without their knowledge. The camera shows its status and a responsible-use notice on screen; the brightness dims after a few seconds without touch, but the screen never turns off.

Free, and “No Banner”

Free with ads; watching, recording and notifications are not paid features. The one-time “No Banner” purchase (€1.99, no subscription) removes the banner in the viewer and on the home screen. The camera screen keeps its banner even with “No Banner”, and when you stop a recording from the viewer a full-screen ad may appear, at most one every 90 seconds.

What happens if the network doesn't let two phones connect directly?

Some networks (corporate Wi-Fi, mobile data behind CGNAT) block direct connections. CamPhone then uses a Cloudflare relay server that forwards the video packets already encrypted with DTLS-SRTP, unable to view them; if it still won't connect, try another Wi-Fi or mobile data.
