What is CamPhone

CamPhone is an iPhone and iPad app that turns a device you no longer use into a security camera, baby monitor or pet cam, and lets you watch it live from another iPhone or iPad. The two are linked with a 10-symbol code, with no accounts or sign-up. Picture and sound go directly from one device to the other, encrypted, and recordings are stored on the camera.

Quick facts

  • Price: free with ads (one banner). Optional one-time “No Banner” purchase for €1.99, no subscription.
  • Devices: iPhone and iPad, in any combination of camera and viewer.
  • Accounts: none. They are linked with a 10-symbol code.
  • Cloud: none. Video goes directly between the two devices and recordings stay on the camera.
  • Languages: Spanish and English.

How it works

  1. Choose “Camera” on the old iPhone or iPad: it shows a 10-symbol code. Leave it plugged in with the app open.
  2. Choose “Viewer” on your other iPhone or iPad and type the code once.
  3. Watch live, with sound, pick the quality and record whenever you want; recordings stay on the camera.

Spare phone as a baby monitor, pet cam or security camera

Use a spare phone as a baby monitor, a pet cam, a home security camera or a small-business camera: anywhere an old iPhone or iPad, plugged in, can watch while you see the picture from your own phone.

What it does

  • Camera: leave it plugged in with the app open. The sensor is off while it waits and turns on only when the viewer connects.
  • Viewer: type the code once and watch live, with sound, pick the quality and switch between the rear and front camera.
  • Recordings: ask for them from the viewer; they are stored on the camera device, and you can watch them from the viewer or move them to the photo library.
  • Secure code: 10 random symbols; “Change code” locks the viewer out instantly.

What it does NOT do

  • It does not detect motion.
  • It does not send notifications.
  • It does not upload video to any cloud: recordings stay on the camera.
  • It is not a substitute for supervision by a person or for a professional security system.

Requirements

  • One iPhone or iPad as the camera and another as the viewer, in any combination.
  • iOS does not allow the camera to be used with the app closed or in the background: the camera device must be plugged in, with the screen on and CamPhone open in front.
  • The camera screen never turns off: after 20 seconds without a touch it drops to a low but visible brightness, and a touch brings it back. The camera's banner always stays in view, even with “No Banner”.
  • Internet on both devices (wifi or mobile data). Only one viewer can be connected to each camera.

Responsible use

Meant for watching your home, business or pets with the knowledge of anyone who appears, not for recording people without their knowledge. The camera shows its status and a responsible-use notice on screen; the brightness dims after a few seconds without touch, but the screen never turns off.

Free, with a one-time “No Banner” purchase

Free with ads. The one-time “No Banner” purchase (€1.99, no subscription) removes the banner in the viewer and on the home screen. The camera screen keeps its banner even with “No Banner”, and when you stop a recording from the viewer a full-screen ad may appear, at most one every 90 seconds.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use an old iPhone as a security camera?

With CamPhone: install it on the old iPhone (Camera mode) and on your own phone (Viewer mode), link them with a 10-symbol code and watch live video and sound. No account or subscription; free with ads.

Is there a phone-to-phone baby monitor app with no account?

Yes: CamPhone links two iPhones or iPads with a code, no sign-up, and streams picture and sound directly between them, encrypted and not stored on any server. The camera device must be plugged in with the app open in front.

What is a security camera app with no cloud or subscription?

CamPhone: video goes directly between your two devices, recordings stay on the camera phone, and there is no subscription; an optional one-time €1.99 purchase only removes the banner.

Can I use an old iPhone as a pet cam for my dog or cat?

Yes. With CamPhone you leave the old iPhone or iPad plugged in with the app open as a camera for your dog or cat, and watch live, with sound, from your other device. It works the same for the baby, the house or a business.

Where are the recordings stored?

On the device that acts as the camera, never on the viewer or in a cloud. From the viewer you can watch them playing from the camera, and on the camera you can move them to the photo library; it keeps up to 6 GB and deletes the oldest when full.

Does it work over mobile data or from another city?

Yes. Both devices need internet, over wifi or mobile data, but they do not have to be on the same wifi. If the network does not allow a direct connection, a relay server forwards the packets, which are already encrypted. Live video uses data, and you can pick low, medium or high quality.

Does it drain the battery?

While nobody is watching, the camera is not open: it runs cooler and uses less battery, and the sensor turns on only when the viewer connects or when recording. The camera screen never turns off: after 20 seconds without a touch it drops to a low but visible brightness, and a touch brings it back. The device should be plugged in.

Can I watch with an iPad?

Yes. CamPhone works between iPhone and iPad in any combination, as camera or as viewer, in portrait or landscape.

Is it safe? Who can see my video, and can I lock out someone who has the code?

Picture and sound go directly from the camera to the viewer, encrypted with DTLS-SRTP (the standard WebRTC encryption), and are not uploaded to any cloud. Only someone with the code can watch, one viewer at a time; “Change code” renews it and the previous viewer cannot get in.

Is it legal to record with it?

CamPhone is meant for watching your home, business or pets with the knowledge of anyone who appears, not for recording people without their knowledge. Whoever uses the camera is responsible for complying with the law of their country.
