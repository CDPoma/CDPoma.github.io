Control Parental Familiar · Help and support

Summary: Control Parental Familiar has no accounts and asks for no email. The server (Supabase) keeps only each device with its token, the last location sent by the child's phone, the battery level, the time allowance status, the settings and the alerts. We do not read messages, we do not use the microphone or the camera and we cannot see the screen. Location is used only for alerts and safe zones. Screen time is measured by Apple's Screen Time inside the child's phone and does not leave it. Google AdMob ads and Firebase Analytics run only on the parent's phone and with your consent; the child's phone has no ads and no analytics. You can delete your data from the app with "Delete my data from the server". We do not sell your data.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Gabriel Mico Victoria, developer of Control Parental Familiar. For any question or request about your data write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com or use the support page.

2. How it works and who uses each phone

The app is installed on two phones and, when opened, a role is chosen: "parent's phone" (the caring adult) or "child's phone". Whoever installs the app on a child's phone must be their parent or guardian, who creates the PIN and is responsible for using it appropriately with the child. The two phones are linked with a 6-digit code; there is no account, email or password.

3. What data we keep on the server

  • One device per phone: its identifier, the role (parent or child), the device name, the app version, the language and, if you accept alerts, the push notification identifier. The phone identifies itself with a random token; the server keeps only its hash, not the token.
  • The links between parents' and children's phones and the name the parent gives each child.
  • The last location the child's phone sends in its "heartbeat" (the periodic signal that it is active) and when it was sent. We do not keep a history of routes.
  • The battery level and whether protection is on.
  • The time allowance status: time granted, time used and whether it is active or used up.
  • The settings the parent sets: blocked websites, safe zones, the AirTag name and the PIN hash, never the PIN itself.
  • The alerts to the parent (SOS, fall, signal lost, low battery, leaving and arriving at zones, protection off), with the last known location at that moment.

The blocked apps are chosen on the child's phone and are not uploaded to the server.

4. What we do NOT collect

We do not read the child's messages or conversations, we do not use the microphone or the camera, we cannot see the child's screen or browsing history, and we do not ask for a real name, email, phone number or contacts.

5. Location

The app asks for the "Always" location permission on the child's phone so it can alert the parent even when the app is in the background. Location is used only for alerts and safe zones (leaving or arriving at a zone, SOS, fall, signal lost) and to show the parent the last known location on the map. We do not use it for advertising or analytics.

6. Apple Screen Time

To measure the allowance, block apps and websites and prevent apps from being deleted, the app uses Apple's Screen Time on the child's phone. Apple processes those choices inside the phone itself: the app does not receive which apps the child uses or their names, and does not send them to any server. The server only receives how much time has been used and whether the allowance is still active.

7. Ads and analytics (parent's phone only)

The parent's phone, without Pro, shows Google AdMob ads: a banner and a rewarded ad when leaving a child's page. Before that, the app shows Google's consent form and, on iPhone, Apple's tracking prompt; if you do not accept, ads do not use your data for personalization. We measure app usage with Firebase Analytics (Google) only on the parent's phone, after your consent and without the advertising identifier, with events such as "ad viewed" or "Pro purchased". The child's phone has no ads, no analytics, and does not ask for the tracking permission or the ads consent form.

8. Control Parental Pro subscription

Apple handles payment: we neither see nor store your card. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have Pro: it receives an app user identifier and the status of your purchase. If you change phone or reinstall, you get Pro back with "Restore purchases". You can cancel in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

9. Notifications

Alerts to the parent arrive as Apple push notifications. We keep the parent phone's notification identifier to be able to send them, and we send a silent notification to the child's phone so it picks up settings changes. You can turn notifications off in iPhone settings.

10. Where it is stored

Server data is stored with Supabase. Google (ads and analytics) and RevenueCat are processors that may handle the data listed above, with the safeguards each one offers. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for other purposes.

11. How long we keep it and how to delete it

  • Alerts: deleted after 90 days.
  • Inactive devices: a device that gives no signal for 180 days is deleted from the server with its data.
  • Deletion from the app: in settings, "Delete my data from the server" removes that phone from the server with its links, status, settings and, for a child, its alerts. On the child's phone the adult's PIN is asked for. You do not need to ask support.

12. Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, to correct it, to delete it, to limit its use, to receive it in a portable format or to object to its processing. Write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com. If you believe we have not respected your rights, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency.

13. Children

The app is meant for an adult to look after the phone of a child in their family. The child does not create an account or give their name, email or phone number; the data from the child's phone is only that in section 3, and that phone has no ads or analytics. The parent or guardian can delete it at any time.

14. Changes

If we change this policy in a significant way, we will tell you in the app.
