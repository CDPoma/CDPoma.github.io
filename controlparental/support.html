Control Parental Familiar · Privacy policy

Important: Control Parental Familiar is a tool for an adult to look after the phone of a child in their family. It does not read messages, use the microphone or camera, or see the screen. It does not replace adult supervision and is not an emergency service: in real danger, call your local emergency number.

Something not working or have a question? Write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com. Tell me the iPhone model, the iOS version and what you were doing.

Frequently asked questions

How do I link the child's phone with mine?

1) On the child's phone, open the app and choose the "child's phone" role. An adult creates the PIN and the app shows a large 6-digit code with a 10-minute countdown and "Get a new code". 2) On the parent's phone, choose "Add a child", type the code and the child's name. From then on you see them in your list. There are no accounts, emails or passwords. To link another adult's phone, on the child's phone and with the PIN, use "Link another phone" to ask for a new code.

What is the PIN and how do I change it?

It is a 4 to 6 digit PIN created by the adult on the child's phone. Without it, the child cannot change settings, unlink or delete the data. Change it in the child's phone settings in "Adult settings" › "Change the PIN". Keep it safe.

How does the daily screen-time allowance work?

From the child's page, the parent sets how much usage time they get per day (for example 5 or 6 hours). When it runs out, the chosen apps and websites are cut off until the next day, and the parent can grant more time or cut it off now. On iPhone it is measured with Apple's Screen Time, inside the child's phone.

How do I block websites?

On the child's page, in blocked websites, add the domain (for example example.com). It is applied on the child's phone at its next heartbeat or immediately with the silent notification.

How do I choose the blocked apps?

Blocked apps are chosen on the child's phone, with the adult's PIN, using Apple's Screen Time picker. The list is not uploaded to the server: it stays on that phone.

What is the app deletion lock?

With protected mode on, the child cannot delete apps from the phone without the adult's PIN. Because of how Apple's Screen Time works, the lock applies to all apps at once, not to a single one. If an app has to be deleted, the adult can use "Allow deleting apps for 10 minutes" with the PIN, a 10-minute permission after which it locks again (or "Lock again now"). If someone removes the Screen Time permission from the app in Settings, the app detects it and the parent gets an alert. For extra safety you can also set a Screen Time passcode in the child's phone settings.

How do I create safe zones?

On the child's page, add a zone (home, school…) with "Add zone at the center of the map", with its name and radius. You will get an alert when the child leaves it and when they arrive. The app needs the "Always" location permission on the child's phone.

How do SOS and fall detection work?

The child asks for help from the app's SOS button, from Shortcuts, with Siri or with the Action Button, and the parent gets an alert with the last location. If the iPhone detects a fall, a countdown with "I'm OK" appears: if it is not cancelled, the parent is alerted.

I am not getting alerts

On the parent's phone, check that the app's notifications are on (iPhone Settings › Notifications) and that no Focus mode is silencing them. On the child's phone, check that location is set to "Always" and that there is a connection. If the child's phone stops giving signals, the parent gets a signal-lost alert.

How do I see where the child is, and what is the AirTag for?

The map on the child's page shows the last known location sent by the child's phone. If the child carries an AirTag, switch on the AirTag option and enter the name it has in Find My: "View and track their AirTag in Find My" opens the Find My app.

What does Control Parental Pro remove? How do I cancel it?

Pro removes the banner and the rewarded ad on the parent's phone. It costs 1,99 € a month or 14,99 € a year in Spain, with 7 days free. It is bought in the parent's settings, where "Restore purchases" is also found with the same Apple ID. To cancel: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Control Parental Pro › Cancel Subscription, at least 24 hours before the period ends; the app cannot cancel it for you, Apple handles it. The child's phone never has ads and does not need Pro.

How do I delete my data?

In the app's settings, "Delete my data from the server". It removes that phone from the server with its links, status, settings and alerts; on the child's phone the PIN is asked for. Alerts are deleted automatically after 90 days and inactive devices after 180.

Which languages and devices does it work on?

On iPhone with iOS 15 or later, with an internet connection. Languages: Spanish (Spain and Latin America), English, Brazilian Portuguese, German, French and Italian.
