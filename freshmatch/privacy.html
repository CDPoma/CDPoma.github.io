FreshMatch · Help and support

Summary: to use FreshMatch you create an account with your email and a name or nickname. We keep your approximate location, rounded to about 1,000 meters (never the exact one), the listings you post with their photos and texts, your messages, your ratings and, if you turn alerts on, your phone's notification identifier. Everything is stored with Supabase, on servers in the European Union, and we do not sell your data. We measure usage with Firebase Analytics without the advertising identifier and only after your consent, we show Google AdMob ads after Google's consent form and we use RevenueCat to check whether you have FreshMatch Pro. You can delete your account from Settings without asking anyone. FreshMatch is for people aged 16 or over.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your data is Gabriel Mico Victoria, the developer of FreshMatch. For any question, report or request about your data write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com or use the support page.

2. What data we keep

  • Your email and your password (encrypted), for your account.
  • Your name or nickname and, if you add them, your city, your description and your profile photo.
  • Your approximate location, rounded to about 1,000 meters (1 km). We never store or show your exact location.
  • The listings you post, with their photos, quantities and prices.
  • The messages you send and receive in conversations.
  • The ratings you give and receive, the kilos saved and the deals done.
  • The reports you make and the people you block.
  • Your device's push notification identifier (token), only if you turn message alerts on.

3. What we use it for

To make the app work: create your account, show you nearby listings, let you talk by messages, alert you when someone writes to you, show ratings and achievements, and moderate content (reports and blocks). We do not sell your data.

4. Your location

The app asks for permission to use your phone's location only to sort listings by distance. If you prefer not to give it, you can pick your city by hand. Other people only see your city and the approximate distance, for example "3 km away".

5. What other people see

Your public profile shows your name, your photo, your city, your description, your kilos saved, your deals, your ratings and your active listings. Your email and your approximate location are not shown. Your messages are only seen by the two people in each conversation. Listing and profile photos are kept in storage that is reached by link, so whoever sees the listing or the profile can see them.

6. Usage analytics

We measure how the app is used (screens visited, errors) with Firebase Analytics, from Google, to improve it. It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not start until you have answered the consent form. We never send your name, your email, the text of your messages or your location. We do not use that data to show you personalized advertising unless you have consented.

7. Ads

FreshMatch shows Google AdMob ads: a banner, an ad when you open the app (1 in 4 opens) and an ad you watch to post a listing. Before showing them, the app shows you Google's consent form so you can choose whether to accept personalized ads and, on iPhone and iPad, Apple's prompt to allow tracking. If you do not accept, the ads do not use your data to be personalized. The FreshMatch Pro subscription removes all three ads.

8. FreshMatch Pro subscription

Apple handles the subscription payment: we do not see or store your card. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have Pro: it receives an app user identifier (not your email) and the status of your purchase. You can cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone.

9. Notifications

If you turn alerts on, we keep your device's notification identifier and use it, through Apple's push notification service, to alert you when someone writes to you. The app asks before turning them on and you can turn them off whenever you want in FreshMatch Settings or in your phone's settings.

10. Where your data is stored

Your data, photos and messages are stored with Supabase, on servers in the European Union. Google (analytics and ads) and RevenueCat are processors that may process the data listed above outside the EU, with the safeguards each one provides.

11. How long we keep it and how to delete it

We keep it while you use FreshMatch. You can delete it whenever you want from Settings › Delete my account: your profile, listings, photos, conversations, messages and ratings are deleted, and so is your email-and-password login if the account was created in FreshMatch. If you created it in another of our apps, only your FreshMatch data is deleted and your login remains the one from that other app. You do not need to ask support.

12. Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict its use, to receive it in a portable format or to object to its processing. Write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com or from the support page. If you think we have not respected your rights, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

13. Minors

FreshMatch is not aimed at people under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If we find out an account belongs to someone younger, we close it.

14. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you in the app.
