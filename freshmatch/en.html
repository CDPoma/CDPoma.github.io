What FreshMatch is

FreshMatch puts people in the same area in touch so that what is left over does not go to waste. Whoever has surplus (fruit, vegetables, garden produce, plants, pantry items and more) posts it with photos; whoever is nearby sees it sorted by distance, chats in real time and arranges the deal: for sale at a guide price, free or for swap. When the deal is closed, both people rate each other and the kilos saved add up.

FreshMatch only puts users in touch. It does not take part in the deals, payments or deliveries, and each person is responsible for what they post, sell, give away or swap. Anything illegal (drugs, weapons, medicines, tobacco) and offensive content are forbidden; reports are handled within 24 hours at most.

How it works

  • Post your surplus: Pick the category (fruit, vegetables, garden, plants, pantry or other), add up to 4 photos, the quantity and whether you sell it (at a guide price), give it away or swap it. Each listing lasts 30 days and you can renew it, pause it or mark it as sold out.
  • Find what is nearby: The list is sorted by distance within the radius you choose, with search and filters by category and type. You can save favorites. If you would rather not share your phone location, you pick your city by hand.
  • Real-time chat with alerts: Message the author from the listing and talk in a private conversation, with push alerts when someone writes to you (the app asks before turning them on).
  • Deal done and ratings: When the deal is closed, both people rate each other from 1 to 5 stars with an optional comment. Each profile shows its ratings, the deals done and the kilos saved.
  • Report and block: You can report a listing or a person and block anyone from their profile: they stop seeing your listings and cannot write to you.

Free and Pro

Free to download, with Google AdMob ads: a banner at the bottom, an ad when you open the app (1 in 4 opens, never the first) and an ad you watch to post a listing. FreshMatch Pro, a monthly or yearly App Store subscription (1,99 € a month or 14,99 € a year in Spain; in other countries you see your local price in the app before paying), with a 7-day free trial, removes all three ads. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends, in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Pro is in Settings › FreshMatch Pro, next to "Restore purchases".

Platforms

Works on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 15 or later and needs an internet connection. App languages: Spanish (Spain and Latin America), English, Brazilian Portuguese, German, French, Italian, Japanese and Korean.

Your data

No email or password is asked for: when you open the app an anonymous account tied to your phone is created and you only choose a name or nickname, which is what other people will see. If you delete the app or change phone, you start from scratch; Pro is recovered with "Restore purchases". Your location is rounded to about 1,000 meters: we never store or show the exact one, and other people only see your city and an approximate distance. Data is stored with Supabase, on servers in the European Union, and you can delete your account yourself from Settings. It is all explained in the privacy policy.

What it does not do

  • It does not handle payments or deliveries: whatever is agreed is agreed directly between the people involved.
  • It does not check what is posted or guarantee its quality, condition or legality, or that people are who they say they are.
  • It does not allow posting what the law does not permit between individuals: drugs, weapons, medicines or tobacco.
  • It does not show your exact location to other people.
  • It does not work offline: lists and chats are loaded from the server.

Frequently asked questions

What can I post on FreshMatch?

Surplus fruit, vegetables, garden produce, plants, pantry items and more, to sell at a guide price, give away or swap. Each person is responsible for what they post; anything illegal (drugs, weapons, medicines, tobacco) and offensive content are forbidden.

How do payment and delivery work?

FreshMatch does not handle payments or deliveries. Prices are only a guide and whatever is agreed is agreed by chat, directly between the people involved.

How do other people see my location?

They do not. The app rounds your location to about 1,000 meters and other people only see your city and the approximate distance, for example "3 km away". If you do not want to share your phone location, you can pick your city by hand.

What are the kilos saved?

When a deal is closed you can say how many kilos were put to use. They add up on your profile, together with the deals done and your ratings.

Which ads does it show and what does Pro remove?

Without Pro there is a banner, an ad when you open the app (1 in 4 opens, never the first) and an ad you watch to post a listing. FreshMatch Pro removes all three; it costs 1,99 € a month or 14,99 € a year in Spain and includes a 7-day free trial.

Do I need an email or a password?

No. When you open the app for the first time an anonymous account tied to your phone is created by itself; you only type a name or nickname and accept the terms. If you delete the app or change phone, you start from scratch; if you were Pro, you get it back with "Restore purchases".

How do I delete my account?

From the app itself: Settings › Delete my account. Your profile, listings, photos, conversations, messages and ratings are deleted. You do not need to ask support.

How old do I have to be?

At least 16. If we find out an account belongs to someone younger, we close it.
