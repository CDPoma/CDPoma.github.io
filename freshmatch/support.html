FreshMatch · Privacy policy

Important: FreshMatch only puts users in touch. It does not take part in the deals, payments or deliveries, and each person is responsible for what they post, sell, give away or swap.

FreshMatch is the app to post your surplus fruit, vegetables, garden produce, plants and pantry items and to find the ones in your area, with chat and ratings. Something not working, want to report content or make a request about your data? Write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

How do I post a listing?

In the "Post" tab: pick the category, add up to 4 photos (the first is the cover), the quantity and whether you sell it (at a guide price), give it away or swap it. Without Pro, an ad is shown before posting; with Pro it posts straight away. Each listing lasts 30 days: you can renew it, pause it or mark it as sold out from "My surplus" (in your profile).

How do I find listings in my area?

In the "Nearby" tab they are sorted by distance. Tap the location chip to change the search radius; you can search by text, filter by category and type, and save favorites. If you have not allowed location, pick your city by hand.

Can I post anything?

No. Anything illegal is forbidden, in particular what the law does not permit between individuals: drugs and narcotic substances, weapons and ammunition, medicines and tobacco. You also cannot post false details, photos that are not yours or offensive content. Each person is responsible for what they post.

How are listings paid for and delivered?

FreshMatch does not handle payments or deliveries: prices are only a guide and whatever is agreed is agreed by chat, directly between the people involved.

How do I close a deal and rate the other person?

In the conversation, tap "Deal done" (you can say how many kilos were put to use). Then both people can rate each other from 1 to 5 stars and leave a comment. Ratings, deals and kilos saved show on the profile.

I am not getting message alerts

Alerts are optional: the app asks after your first message and you can turn them on or off in Settings › Notifications. If they show as "Blocked", turn them on in iPhone Settings › Notifications › FreshMatch.

How do I report a listing or a person? And how do I block?

From the menu of the listing, the conversation or the profile: "Report" (pick the reason and add details if you like) or "Block". A blocked person stops seeing your listings and cannot write to you; you can unblock them in Settings › Blocked users. We review every report and remove what breaks the terms within 24 hours at most; when several different people report a listing, it is suspended until it is reviewed.

How do I delete my account?

From the app: Settings › Delete my account, and confirm twice. Your profile, listings, photos, conversations, messages and ratings are deleted, and so is your anonymous account. You do not need to ask support.

What happens if I change phone or delete the app?

FreshMatch does not ask for an email or a password: your account is anonymous and tied to your phone. If you delete the app or change phone, you start from scratch with a new account (your listings and chats from the old one are not carried over). If you were Pro, you get it back in Settings › FreshMatch Pro › "Restore purchases" with the same Apple ID. If you want us to delete the old account, write to us.

What does FreshMatch Pro remove? How do I cancel or restore it?

Pro removes the banner, the ad when you open the app and the ad to post. It costs 1,99 € a month or 14,99 € a year in Spain, with a 7-day free trial. It is in Settings › FreshMatch Pro, where "Restore purchases" is too if you were already Pro with the same Apple ID. To cancel: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the period ends; FreshMatch cannot cancel it for you, Apple manages it.

Which languages and devices does it support?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 15 or later, with an internet connection. Languages: Spanish (Spain and Latin America), English, Brazilian Portuguese, German, French, Italian, Japanese and Korean; change it in Settings › Language.

How old do I have to be?

At least 16. If we find out an account belongs to someone younger, we close it.
