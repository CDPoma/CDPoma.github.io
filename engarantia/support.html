Help and support

EnGarantia · Privacy policy

Mind the periods: EnGarantia works out the warranty end date from an indicative period (3 years in Spain, 2 in the rest of the EU and EEA, 1 in other countries). It is not legal advice: always check your receipt and the manufacturer's warranty.

EnGarantia keeps your receipts and warranties: you scan the receipt with the camera, it saves it as a PDF, AI fills in the details, works out when the warranty ends and reminds you a month before, with the manual and the contacts to make a claim. Something not working or have an idea? Write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

How do I scan a receipt?

On Home, tap "Scan receipt". The first time, the app explains the AI reading and asks for your permission; then the camera opens (the system asks for camera permission). Frame the receipt with the paper flat and good light, take the photo and, if it has more sheets, "Another page" (up to 3). When you finish, tap "Done". On an Apple silicon Mac the computer's camera is used: hold the paper in front of it.

Why can't I pick a photo from my library or a PDF?

It is on purpose: EnGarantia only accepts photos taken at that moment with the camera so what you keep is the original receipt and not an edited image. If you scan another device's screen, the app detects it and asks you to scan the paper.

The AI misread a detail or read nothing

Always check the details before saving: you can correct product, brand, model, store, date, price and years. If the photo was blurry or cut off, scan again in good light with the paper flat. Offline, or if you have reached the day's limit, you can tap "Fill in by hand". You can edit the warranty later from its page.

How do I change the years of warranty?

When checking the details, in "Years of warranty" (and "Extra months", if needed). The app suggests the years from the country on the receipt: 3 in Spain, 2 in the rest of the EU and EEA, 1 elsewhere. You can also change them later with "Edit" on the product page. It is an indicative period: check your receipt and the manufacturer's warranty.

I am not getting the end-of-warranty reminder

The reminder is a local notification, a month before it ends, at the time you choose in Settings (10:00 by default). Check that EnGarantia notifications are on in iPhone Settings › Notifications › EnGarantia, and that the warranty has not already expired (there is no reminder for a past warranty). The product page says when you will be reminded or whether the reminder is off. With many warranties, iOS keeps a maximum of pending notifications and the app reschedules the nearest ones when it opens.

How do I claim the warranty?

Open the product page. Under "Claim the warranty" you will see the customer service phone, email and website of the brand and of the store, with buttons to call or send a ready-written email with the purchase details. Open the receipt PDF from the page and attach it to the email. The brand's contacts are found by the AI on the web and the store's come from what is printed on the receipt: check them before claiming, and if one is missing, you can type it yourself.

How does it find the manual? What is "approximate"?

After saving, the app searches the web for the user manual of your brand and model. "Official" means the address belongs to the brand; "approximate" means it is the closest match found (a similar model or a page that is not the brand's). If it finds none, you can search again from the page. An internet connection is needed.

How do I share or export the PDFs?

On a product page, "View receipt (PDF)" opens it and from there you can share it by email or messaging. In Settings › "Export all PDFs" the share sheet opens with all the PDFs at once, to save them in Files or send them.

What happens to my data if I change phone or delete the app?

Your warranties, PDFs and photos are only on the device, with no account or copy on our server. If you delete the app or change device without a system backup, they are lost: before that, export the PDFs from Settings. The privacy policy explains it in detail.

What are the ads for? How do I remove them? What is EnGarantia Pro?

The free version shows a banner, a full-screen ad while the AI reads the receipt and, on 1 in 5 app opens, another when you open the app (never on the day you install). EnGarantia Pro is a monthly or yearly App Store subscription (with a 7-day free trial the first time) that removes all ads and raises the daily limit from 20 reads and 10 manual searches to 60 and 30. You get it in Settings › "Go Pro", where "Restore purchases" is also found if you were already Pro on another device with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel the subscription?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › EnGarantia Pro › Cancel subscription, at least 24 hours before the period or the trial ends. EnGarantia cannot cancel it for you: Apple manages it. If you cancel, you keep Pro until the end of the paid period.

It says I have reached the day's limit

Without Pro there are up to 20 AI receipt reads and 10 manual searches a day; with Pro, 60 and 30. The counter resets every day (UTC). Meanwhile you can save warranties by filling in the details by hand.

Which devices and languages does it support?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 15 or later and, as an iPad app, Apple silicon Macs. It is in Spanish (Spain and Latin America), English, Brazilian Portuguese, German, French and Italian; change it in Settings › Language.
