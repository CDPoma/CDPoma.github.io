What EnGarantia is

EnGarantia keeps track of the warranties of what you buy. You scan the receipt or invoice with the camera, the app saves it as a PDF with the original photo, artificial intelligence (Claude, by Anthropic) reads the details, works out when the warranty ends and reminds you a month before. If something breaks, the receipt, the manual and the contacts to make a claim are one tap away. It works without an account: your warranties, PDFs and photos are stored on your device.

Important: the warranty period EnGarantia works out is a guide (it depends on the country and the product) and is not legal advice. Always check your receipt and the manufacturer's warranty. A manual marked "approximate" is not the official one for your model.

What you get

  • Scan the receipt, never lose it: A document scanner with the camera (up to 3 pages per receipt): frame the paper and it crops and straightens it for you. There is no photo library or file import, on purpose: live camera only, so what you keep is the original receipt. The PDF carries the scan date and time and a digital fingerprint (SHA-256) of every page in its footer, and the original photo is kept too, with zoom.
  • AI fills in the details: Claude reads the store, purchase date, product, brand, model, price and country, and recognizes the type of product. It warns you if the receipt looks like a photo of a screen or an edited image. You check and correct everything before saving; offline, you fill it in by hand.
  • A reminder before it runs out: Works out the warranty end date from the years it suggests for the country of purchase (3 in Spain, 2 in the rest of the EU and EEA, 1 elsewhere); you can change them and add extra months. A local notification a month before, at the time you choose in Settings.
  • All your purchases at a glance: A grid with one icon per product type (phone, TV, washer, laptop, headphones…). Each icon shows how much warranty is left by color: green, amber, red or expired.
  • Manual and claim, on hand: Finds the official user manual for your model, or the closest match, and tells you whether it is "official" or "approximate". It shows the customer service phone, email and website of both the brand and the store, with buttons to call or send a ready-written email to make your warranty claim. Open the receipt PDF and share it, or export every PDF at once from Settings.

Free and Pro

Free to download, with Google AdMob ads: a banner at the bottom, a full-screen ad while the AI reads the receipt (with the notice "This ad is shown while your receipt is being read") and, now and then (1 in 5 app opens, never on the day you install), one when you open the app. Up to 20 AI receipt reads and 10 manual searches a day. EnGarantia Pro, a monthly or yearly App Store subscription (in Spain 1,99 € a month or 14,99 € a year; in other countries you see your local price in the app before paying), with a 7-day free trial the first time, removes all ads and raises the limit to 60 reads and 30 manual searches a day. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends, in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Pro is in Settings › "Go Pro".

Platforms

Works on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 15 or later, and on Apple silicon Macs as an iPad app (on a Mac it scans with the computer's camera). An internet connection is needed to read the receipt with AI and to look for the manual and the contacts; without it you can fill in the details by hand. App languages: Spanish (Spain and Latin America), English, Brazilian Portuguese, German, French and Italian.

Your data

There is no account or sign-up: your warranties, PDFs and photos are stored on your device. The receipt photos are sent to our server (Supabase, in the European Union) and to Anthropic (Claude) only to be read, and are not stored. Looking for the manual and the brand's contacts uses Claude's web search with the brand and model. It is all explained in the privacy policy.

What it does not do

  • It does not import receipts from the photo library, from files or from other apps: you can only scan with the live camera.
  • It does not give legal advice: the warranty period is a suggestion you check against your receipt and the manufacturer's warranty.
  • It does not make the claim for you: it gives you the contacts and a ready-written email, and you send it.
  • It does not keep your receipts on our server or sync them between devices: they stay on the device where you scan them.
  • It cannot read a receipt or look for a manual without an internet connection.
  • It does not promise the manual is the official one: it says "official" only when the address belongs to the brand and "approximate" otherwise.

Frequently asked questions

Can I import a receipt from my photo library or a PDF?

No, on purpose. EnGarantia only accepts photos taken at that moment with the camera, so what you keep is the original receipt and not an edited image. If you scan another device's screen, the AI detects it and the app asks you to scan the paper.

Which AI reads the receipt and what happens to my photo?

The artificial intelligence is Claude, by Anthropic, called from a function on our server (Supabase, in the European Union), not from your device. The photo is used only to read the receipt and is not kept on our server or in the logs. The first time you scan, the app explains it and asks for your permission.

How many years of warranty does it work out?

The ones it suggests for the country on the receipt: 3 years in Spain, 2 in the rest of the European Union and EEA, and 1 in other countries. It is a starting point: you can change the years and add extra months when you save. A brand's commercial warranties are not worked out on their own.

How does it remind me?

With a local notification a month before each warranty ends, at the time you choose in Settings (10:00 by default). It is scheduled on your device and does not go through any server; the app asks for notification permission when you save your first warranty.

How does it find the manual and the brand's contacts? Can I trust them?

A function on our server asks Claude, with its web search, for the manual of your brand and model and for the brand's support page. Only addresses that really appeared in the results and respond are shown, and only phone numbers and emails that appear verbatim on those pages. Still, check them before claiming; the store's contact comes from what is printed on your receipt.

Is it free? What does Pro include and what if I change phone?

Free to download, with every feature in the free version, with ads and a daily limit of reads and searches. EnGarantia Pro removes the ads and raises the limit. Since there is no account or copy on our server, your warranties stay on the device: before changing phone or deleting the app, export the PDFs from Settings.
