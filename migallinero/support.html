My Coop is the backyard-chicken notebook for your phone: hens, eggs, eggs given out, feed and water. Something not working, or an idea? Write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to sign up?

No. There are no accounts or email: open the app and start. Your data is stored on your phone.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Everything is inside the app, so you can log eggs in the coop with no network. Only «My Farm» needs a connection to sync.

I changed phones or uninstalled the app: do I lose my data?

Data lives on the phone, so it is lost on uninstall. Beforehand, make a backup (Settings → export) and save the file wherever you like; on the new phone, import it. With «My Farm» (Pro) the data is also in the cloud and comes back when you join with your code.

How do I share the coop with my family?

With a Pro subscription: in Settings → My Farm create a farm and give the code to the others, who type it in «Join a farm». Everyone picks their own profile name. What one person logs, the others see.

I do not get feed and water reminders

Check that My Coop notifications are allowed in your phone settings. Reminders are scheduled on the phone itself; anything falling between 10 pm and 8 am is moved to 9 pm. On some Android phones the app must be exempt from battery restrictions.

I bought Pro and it does not show

Go to Settings → My Coop Pro → Restore purchases. The store sometimes takes a few minutes to confirm payment. If it still does not appear, write to us with your Apple or Google receipt.

How do I cancel the subscription?

From your store account: on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

How do I delete my data?

On the phone, uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on it. If you used «My Farm», go to Settings → My Farm → «Delete farm from the cloud»: the farm's cloud data is truly deleted (each phone keeps its own). If you no longer have the app, write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com with your farm code and we will delete it for you. More details in the privacy policy.
