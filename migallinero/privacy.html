My Coop

Privacy Policy (Spanish name: Mi Gallinero)

HomeHelp & support

Summary: My Coop keeps everything on your phone. There are no accounts and it never asks for your email, real name, location or contacts. Only if you turn on «My Farm» (Pro) is the coop data also stored in Google's cloud (Firebase, servers in the European Union) under a farm code that you share yourself. The free version shows Google AdMob ads, personalized only if you consent. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google.

Who is responsible

Gabriel Micó Victoria, developer of My Coop. For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights, write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Hens (breed, dates, leg band, notes and photo), eggs collected, eggs given out and their prices, feed records, feed and water levels and settings are stored only in the app's own storage (IndexedDB). They do not leave your phone unless you turn on «My Farm». If you uninstall the app they are deleted with it.

  • Hen photos. When you choose «Camera» or «Gallery» to set a hen's photo, the app asks for permission and uses the image only for that: it crops it, shrinks it (about 400 pixels) and stores it inside the app. The camera and gallery are not used for anything else.
  • Feed and water reminders. These are local notifications scheduled by your own phone from the estimated remaining supply. There is no notification server and nothing is sent for this. If you do not grant permission, the app works the same, without reminders.
  • Backup. If you export your data, the app creates a file and lets you choose where to save it or who to share it with. We never receive it.

«My Farm» (Pro): data in the cloud

If, with a Pro subscription, you create a farm or join someone else's, the coop data is also stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), on servers in the European Union (eur3), so every phone in the farm sees it right away. What is synchronized:

  • hens, including their photo;
  • eggs collected, eggs given out and their prices;
  • feed records, feed and water levels, and the coop settings;
  • the profile name each person chooses on joining (we recommend a nickname, not a full name).

No accounts, no email. A farm is identified by a 10-character code generated by the app. Anyone who has the code can see and change that farm's data, so share it only with people you trust. There is no way to list or search farms.

Google, as the cloud provider, may process technical connection data (such as the IP address) to provide the service, under its own policy. We do not use farm data for advertising and we do not sell it.

Stop sharing and delete the cloud data

  • Delete farm from the cloud (Settings → My Farm → «Delete farm from the cloud»): truly deletes all of the farm's data stored in Firebase (hens with their photos, eggs, eggs given out, feed records, levels, settings and profiles). Only a «farm closed» marker remains, with no data or names, so the other phones in the farm stop syncing and the code cannot be used again. The data stays on each phone; the deletion affects only the cloud copy.
  • Leaving the farm (Settings → My Farm) stops sharing: your phone stops sending and receiving. Your data stays on your phone, and the cloud data stays there while other people remain in the farm; if you are the last one, use «Delete farm from the cloud» first.
  • Everyday single deletions (a hen, an entry…): when you delete one in the app, its cloud record is replaced by an empty marker (its identifier, the deleted flag and the date, with no content) so the other phones delete it too.
  • Alternatively, you can write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com with the farm code and we will delete it for you. Without the code we cannot locate it, because there are no accounts.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows a banner from Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before requesting any ad; on iPhone it also asks for Apple's tracking permission (ATT). If you consent, Google may show personalized ads and use the device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, advertising ID on Android), together with technical data such as the IP address and phone model. If you do not, ads still appear but are not personalized. Google does not receive your coop data: no hens, eggs or photos.

You can change your choice at any time: on iPhone, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android, in your phone's Google settings (Ads), where you can delete or reset the advertising ID.

In-app purchases

The Pro subscription (€1.99/month or €14.99/year) is charged by Apple or Google through your store account; we never see payment details. RevenueCat verifies the subscription with the store and tells the app whether you are Pro; for that it knows a random installation identifier and your purchase status. You can cancel at any time from your Apple or Google subscription settings. «Restore purchases» is on the Pro screen (Settings → My Coop Pro).

Children and your rights

My Coop is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. At any time you can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, and exercise the rest of your GDPR rights (access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability) by writing to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

Legal basis: data is processed to provide the app you asked for (performance of the service); personalized ads, with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time; and non-personalized ads, under our legitimate interest in funding the free version.

Security

Communication with the cloud is encrypted (HTTPS). The server rules only allow reading and writing a farm to someone who knows its exact code, and do not allow listing farms.
