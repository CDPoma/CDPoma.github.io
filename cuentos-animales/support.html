Help and support

Privacy policy

Animal Stories for Kids is an interactive picture book for children aged 2 to 6: every page is an animal in its home, and tapping anything says its name in the chosen language. Something not working? Write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

How does it work?

Choose a story on the shelf. Tap an animal and it says its name, then moves and makes its sound. The sun, a flower or the water say their names too. Turn pages with the arrows or by swiping. Every animal you find goes into the sticker album (the star at the top).

What is free?

The first page of each story, in the device language. The four complete stories are unlocked with a single purchase, and each extra language is bought separately or in a pack of 3 languages of your choice.

How do I change the language?

With the flags at the top. The "+" button shows every language: the ones you have and the ones you can buy. If you bought the 3-language pack, tap the ones you want until you have chosen three.

I bought something and it doesn't show

The payment is confirmed with the store and can take a few minutes. If it still doesn't show, go to Parents → Restore purchases.

Why does it ask me a multiplication?

It is the grown-up question: it appears before buying and before entering the Parents area, so a small child cannot buy by accident.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The stories, voices and sounds are inside the app. You only need a connection to buy or restore purchases.
