Animal Stories for Kids

Privacy policy

Help and support

Summary: Animal Stories for Kids (Cuentos de animales) is an interactive picture book for young children. It has no ads, needs no sign-up and does not collect names, email, phone numbers, location, photos, audio or contacts. We use no analytics or tracking. What the child does (the album stickers and the chosen language) is stored only on the device. Purchases are charged by Apple or Google, and a grown-up question appears before any purchase.

Who is responsible

Gabriel Micó Victoria, developer of the app. For any question about data, or to exercise your rights, write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

What is stored and where

Only on the device: the stickers earned in each story, the chosen language, the languages picked with the 3-language pack and whether the first-time hint has been shown. They never leave the device and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

The app has no server of its own, creates no accounts and sends nobody what the child taps, sees or hears. The stories, drawings, voices and sounds are inside the app and work offline.

In-app purchases

The first page of each story is free. The four stories and extra languages are bought inside the app. Before every purchase a grown-up question (a multiplication) appears, so a child cannot buy by accident.

Payment is handled by Apple or Google with the store account; we never see payment details. RevenueCat checks the purchase with the store to know what is unlocked; for that it uses a random identifier created by the app (which identifies nobody) and the purchase history of this app. "Restore purchases" is in the Parents area.

What we don't do

  • We show no ads.
  • We use no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, nor the device advertising identifier.
  • There is no chat, no social media links and no contact with other people.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone.

Children and rights

The app is designed for children aged 2 to 6. That is why it asks for no personal data and everything that costs money is behind a grown-up check. Parents or guardians can write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com with any question or to exercise their GDPR rights (access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability). You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Deleting data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the device. Purchase receipts are kept by Apple or Google in the store account, under their own policies. If you also want the RevenueCat purchase record deleted, write to us.

Changes

If the app changes what it does with data, this page is updated first and the date below changes.
