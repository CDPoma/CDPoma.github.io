Laser Racer

Support and privacy

Quick summary: the game does not ask for or store any personal data. There is no account, sign-up, email or password. What goes over the internet is: ads (Google AdMob), the check of your purchases (RevenueCat and Apple) and, only when needed, the check of the minimum game version and online games with other people (Supabase). Personalised ads are only enabled with your consent where the law requires it.

What the game is

Laser Racer is a fast spaceship game. You fly through canyons and landscapes across five worlds, dodging obstacles, shooting enemies and holding out with your shield. Each world has five levels, the last one with a boss.

Game modes:

  • Campaign: the levels of the five worlds, one after another.
  • With friends: two to four pilots, on the same device or online, with a room code or matched with another player. In Race everyone runs the same stretch, with no health: heat rises with lasers and at the limit the ship drops for a moment.

There are four ships —Halcón, Aguja, Mamut and Cometa—, each with its own handling. You play with the touchscreen or a controller. The game is in Spanish and English.

What is free and what is paid

The download is free and includes ads. The whole game can be played with the Halcón ship, which is free.

There are four purchases, all one-time payments (not subscriptions, they do not renew and nothing else is charged):

  • No banner (€1.99 in Spain; your country's exact price is shown by the store): removes the banner ad. The other ads stay.
  • Aguja ship, Mamut ship and Cometa ship (€0.99 each in Spain): unlock that ship so you can fly it.

Purchases can be restored on another device with the same Apple account (the "Restore purchases" button).

What data is stored and where

No personal data. No name or email is requested and no account is created. The game does not read your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.

Your progress —levels completed, worlds and ships unlocked, language, volume and controls— is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded to any server or synced, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Playing online

The online "With friends" mode and the leaderboards use Supabase, a database and real-time service contracted by the developer. It works like this:

  • Your device opens an anonymous session: a random identifier created when you install the game. It carries no email, name, account or the device's advertising identifier.
  • You do not type your pilot name: the game makes it up from a closed list of words and three digits (for example "Solar Halcón 417"). There is no text field in the game.
  • During a game only each player's controls and the room data are sent: ship, "ready", made-up name and game version. No video, voice or content written by you is sent.
  • There is no chat, neither text nor voice. There is no way to send a message to another player.
  • Leaderboards store the made-up name, the points, the mode, the duration and the game version.
  • As with any internet connection, the server receives your IP address. It is the developer's server: it is not sold or shared with anyone.

If you play alone or with someone on the same device, no connection is needed to play.

Mandatory update

When the game opens it asks Supabase for the minimum supported version (only the game name and platform are sent; the server receives your IP address as in any connection). If your version is older, the game asks you to update from the App Store before continuing. Without a connection you can play normally.

Ads and consent

The game shows Google AdMob ads:

  • a banner at the bottom of the screen, removed by the "No banner" purchase;
  • a full-screen ad before a race starts, with a notice on screen beforehand, at most one every 90 seconds;
  • an app-open ad when you open the game or come back to it after thirty minutes or more away, on one in five launches;
  • an optional rewarded ad: only if you choose to watch it, when you are destroyed, to continue where you were with full health. It never appears on its own.

When you open the game for the first time, and before any ad is requested, this happens, in this order:

  1. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form.
  2. On iPhone and iPad, a short Google screen explaining tracking and then Apple's prompt asking whether you allow the app to track you.
  3. Only then does AdMob start, and only if the consent step allows it.

If you allow it, AdMob may use the device's advertising identifier to personalise and measure ads; if not, ads still appear but are not personalised or are limited to what your choice allows. In both cases Google receives technical device data, such as the IP address. Saying no is perfectly valid: the game works exactly the same.

Change your choice: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the "Ad privacy" button in the game's Settings reopens Google's form. The tracking permission is changed in iPhone or iPad Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The Settings also link to this page.

Permissions

The game asks for no system permission: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or notifications. The only prompt is Apple's tracking prompt described above.

Purchases and refunds

Purchases are one-time payments and do not renew. Billing and refunds are handled by Apple, not the developer. To request a refund: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under thirteen, does not ask for personal data and has no chat or free text field.
