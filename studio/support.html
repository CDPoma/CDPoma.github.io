Studio: features, pricing and FAQ · Privacy policy

Studio: Video Editor is an easy-to-use video editor with professional results that also records your screen. Your videos are edited and saved on your device. Something not working? Write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

How do I trim a video?

Open the video and tap “Cut video”. Two bars appear: leave between them what you want to keep (the green part stays) and tap “Cut”. You will see how it looks; if you like it, “Confirm”; if not, move the bars again. Nothing changes until you confirm, and “Undo” is always at hand.

How do I remove a part from the middle of a video?

Tap “Remove segment”. Leave between the two bars what you want to remove (the red part goes, even in the middle) and tap “Remove”. You will see how it looks without that segment; if you like it, “Confirm”. It is free, like “Cut video”.

How do I join several videos?

Tap “Join videos” (a Pro tool), pick the videos and where each one goes (at the start, after another or at the end). You see them right away on the timeline and they only stay when you tap “Confirm”. Then save the result with “Save video”.

How do I add subtitles?

Tap “Add subtitles” (a Pro tool): the app transcribes the speech of the video on your own device (the audio is not sent to any server). If you add the “Names or words said in the video”, it spells them right; those names stay on your device. Review the text, fix what you need and pick a style.

How do I record my screen?

Tap “Record screen”, grant the recording permission and start. You can turn on the microphone to narrate and the camera for a video reaction. When you finish, the recording opens in the editor and is saved on your device.

Is there a watermark?

No. Saving the video is free, with no watermark and no time limit, as MP4 up to 1080p. Saving in more than 1080p is a Pro feature.

How do I use a Pro tool with an ad?

In the free version: when you tap a Pro tool you can choose “Watch an ad and use [tool] for 20 min”. That ad unlocks only that tool for 20 minutes; each tool needs its own ad and another ad for the same tool adds 20 more minutes. When the time runs out the app asks “Continue?” (ad, Pro or “Not now”); with “Not now” nothing is deleted: your project stays saved half-done and you carry on another day. Cutting never needs an ad. On Mac and PC there are no ads: the computer shows a QR code for that tool, you scan it with Studio on your iPhone, watch the whole ad there and the tool unlocks for 20 minutes on the computer.

How do I ask an AI for changes inside Studio (AI Chat)?

Add the API key of the AI you already use (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or a compatible one) in “Use your own AI”. Then, in any tool, open the “AI Chat” tab and ask for the change in your own words. Your AI proposes it, you see it in the preview and it only stays if you tap “Confirm”. It needs no Pro on iPhone and iPad; on Mac and Windows it needs Pro (or the 7-day trial). Your provider bills you for what you use. What is sent and to whom is in the privacy policy.

What does the “Create with Google AI” button do?

It only opens gemini.google.com in your browser, with your Google account. The app sends no videos or data to Google.

What Pro includes

Pro (€1.99 per month or €14.99 per year) unlocks all tools and saving in more than 1080p, and removes ads on mobile and tablet. There are no ads on Mac and Windows.

How the trial works

After installing the app you get 7 days of full Pro, with no card and no charge. The trial is managed by the app itself from the date of the first download; it is not a purchase or a store trial. When it ends, the app goes back to the free version unless you subscribe: your projects stay where they are.

Restore purchases and cancel

  • Restore: on the Pro screen tap “Restore Purchases”, with the same store account you paid with.
  • iPhone, iPad and Mac (Apple): Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Studio, Cancel.
  • Android (Google Play): Play Store, your profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Studio, Cancel.
  • Windows (Microsoft Store): account.microsoft.com/services, Studio, Cancel.

If you paid and do not have Pro, tap “Restore Purchases”; store confirmation sometimes takes a few minutes. If nothing changes, write to us with your device model and purchase date.

Delete my data

Your videos and projects are only on your device: uninstall the app to delete them. On Android and Windows, the 7-day trial stores on our server an encrypted device identifier and the date of first use. To have it deleted (and, if you wish, your AdMob, Firebase and RevenueCat data), write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com telling us the device model; we will handle it and confirm. More detail in the privacy policy.
