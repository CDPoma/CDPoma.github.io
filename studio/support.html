Help and support

Privacy policy

Studio: Video Editor is an easy video editor for beginners that also records your screen. Your videos are edited and saved on your device. Something not working? Write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

How do I trim a video?

Open the video in a new project, select it on the timeline and drag the ends until only the part you want remains. You can also split it at the playhead and delete the extra piece.

How do I join several videos?

Add all the videos to the project: they are placed one after another on the timeline. Drag them to change the order and trim them if needed before exporting.

How do I add subtitles?

Use the editor's subtitles option: the app transcribes the speech of the video on your own device (the audio is not sent to any server). Review the text, fix what you need and pick a style.

How do I record my screen?

Tap “Record screen”, grant the recording permission and start. You can turn on the microphone to narrate and the camera for a video reaction. When you finish, the recording opens in the editor and is saved on your device.

How do I save without a watermark?

Exporting without a watermark is a Pro feature. It is included during the 7-day trial; afterwards, with the Pro subscription. You can also watch an optional rewarded ad to get all of Pro for 30 minutes.

What does the “Create with Google AI” button do?

It only opens gemini.google.com in your browser, with your Google account. The app sends no videos or data to Google.

What Pro includes

Pro (€1.99 per month) unlocks all features, including export without a watermark, and removes ads on mobile and tablet. There are no ads on Mac and Windows.

How the trial works

After installing the app you get 7 days of full Pro, with no card and no charge. The trial is managed by the app itself from the date of the first download; it is not a purchase or a store trial. When it ends, the app goes back to the free version unless you subscribe: your projects stay where they are.

Restore purchases and cancel

  • Restore: on the Pro screen tap “Restore Purchases”, with the same store account you paid with.
  • iPhone, iPad and Mac (Apple): Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Studio, Cancel.
  • Android (Google Play): Play Store, your profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Studio, Cancel.
  • Windows (Microsoft Store): account.microsoft.com/services, Studio, Cancel.

If you paid and do not have Pro, tap “Restore Purchases”; store confirmation sometimes takes a few minutes. If nothing changes, write to us with your device model and purchase date.
