ItsRain: help and support

Answers to the most common questions.

ItsRain · Privacy policy

Why does ItsRain ask for my location?

To work out the rain and the weather for your exact spot. The calculation is done on your phone, and your location is not stored on any ItsRain server. Details in the privacy policy.

Why doesn't it always warn me when the confidence is low?

Every rain forecast comes with a confidence score. The Live Activity (Lock Screen and Dynamic Island) only appears when rain is less than 60 minutes away and the confidence is at least 65%. With lower confidence, the app does not announce it on the Lock Screen or in the Dynamic Island.

Where does the data come from?

  • Rain radar: AEMET (CC BY 4.0) over mainland Spain and the Balearic Islands; in the rest of the world, satellite and Apple Weather's minute-by-minute forecast.
  • Hourly and daily forecast: Apple Weather and MET Norway (CC BY 4.0).
  • The planet and the layers: Apple's 3D satellite map, clouds from the Meteosat, GOES and Himawari satellites (EUMETSAT, NOAA, JMA), NASA (GIBS, VIIRS and GEOS-CF), Copernicus Sentinel-5P, ECMWF, DWD ICON and AWS Terrain Tiles.

How do I cancel ItsRain Pro?

Cancel in Apple Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions, ItsRain and Cancel. ItsRain Pro costs 0.99 € a month or 9.99 € a year in Spain, with 7 free days for anyone who has not tried it before.

How do I turn off the Live Activity?

Inside ItsRain: open Settings, go to the Alerts section and turn off «Live Activity in the Dynamic Island». It will then stop showing on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Does it work outside Spain?

Yes, worldwide. The AEMET radar covers mainland Spain and the Balearic Islands. In the rest of the world, "Rain in X min" uses Apple Weather's minute-by-minute forecast where it exists and the hourly forecast where it does not; the cloud, rain, wind and storm layers come from satellites and models.
