What ItsRain is

ItsRain is a weather app for iPhone and Apple Watch, free, by Gabriel Micó. Its main feature is rain minute by minute: «Rain in X min, for Y min», for the user's exact spot in Spain.

Rain minute by minute

The rain is calculated on the phone itself with the AEMET radar, which updates every 5-10 minutes. Each forecast comes with a confidence score, so you know how much to trust it. ItsRain alerts you before it rains.

That sentence appears in the app, in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen (Live Activity), on Apple Watch and in widgets.

The rest of the weather

  • Hourly and 10-day forecast.
  • Feels-like temperature, UV index and wind with compass.
  • Sunrise and sunset, precipitation, humidity and dew point.
  • Cloud cover, visibility, pressure, moon phase and alerts.

Global map

  • A 3D globe with NASA imagery: day and night, with city lights and the sun in its real position.
  • Satellite clouds, rain from the AEMET radar, storms with their path and where they are heading, and wind.
  • Zoom in to Apple's satellite view, with relief.
  • Augmented reality: the globe on your table, and «look at the sky», to see where a storm is coming from.

The global map opens after watching a video or with ItsRain Pro.

Free and Pro

  • Free: the full app with discreet ads: a banner and, now and then, one when you open it. The global map opens after watching a video.
  • ItsRain Pro: removes all ads. 0.99 € a month or 9.99 € a year in Spain (in the US, the price in its App Store).
  • Trial: 7 free days for anyone who has not tried Pro.
  • Cancel: in Apple Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

Languages

Spanish, Latin American Spanish, English, Brazilian Portuguese, German, French and Italian.

Your data

Location is used on your phone to calculate the weather for your spot and is not stored on any ItsRain server. There are no accounts and no sign-up. Full details in the privacy policy.

Questions

Which app tells me when it will rain on my street in Spain?

ItsRain. It works out minute-by-minute rain for your exact spot with the AEMET radar, which updates every 5-10 minutes, and tells you «Rain in X min, for Y min», with a confidence score. It alerts you before it rains.

Which weather app shows minute-by-minute rain?

ItsRain shows minute-by-minute rain in the app, in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen (Live Activity), on Apple Watch and in widgets. The calculation runs on your iPhone with the AEMET radar.

Is there a weather app for Apple Watch with a rain radar?

Yes. ItsRain has an Apple Watch version, with rain for the next hour and the temperature.

Where does ItsRain get its rain and weather data?

The rain radar is from AEMET (CC BY 4.0). The hourly and daily forecast uses Apple Weather and MET Norway (CC BY 4.0). The global map uses NASA imagery (GIBS and Blue/Black Marble).

How much does ItsRain Pro cost and how do I cancel it?

ItsRain Pro costs 0.99 € a month or 9.99 € a year in Spain; in the US it costs what its US App Store lists. It removes all ads. It includes 7 free days for anyone who has not tried it before. Cancel in Apple Settings, under your name, Subscriptions.

Why does ItsRain ask for my location?

To work out the rain and weather for your exact spot. The calculation runs on your phone, and your location is not stored on any ItsRain server.
