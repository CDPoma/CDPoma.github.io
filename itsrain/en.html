What ItsRain is

The home screen is the planet: Apple's satellite map in 3D, with relief and night where it really is. Pull up the "Now" sheet for the weather now, hourly and for 10 days, with feels-like temperature, UV, wind, humidity, pressure, visibility, sun and moon, and weather alerts. The layers menu, bottom right, works like a professional weather map and remembers what you choose.

The layers

  • Clouds now: the real picture from the Meteosat, GOES and Himawari satellites, at their height and casting their shadow on the ground; with Play, the last 6 hours.
  • Cloud forecast: the model's cloud cover with the rain on top, up to 5 days; low, mid and high clouds, fog and cloud ceiling.
  • Rain: AEMET radar over mainland Spain and the Balearic Islands, satellite rain for the rest of the world; accumulated rain and snow.
  • Wind: animated particles that follow its direction, coloured by strength, and gusts.
  • Storms: over mainland Spain and the Balearic Islands, the radar's storm cells and where they head in the next hour; elsewhere, the ones the satellite sees.
  • Temperature, feels-like and dew point; air quality (European EAQI and PM2.5 from NASA's GEOS-CF model); sea (waves, swell and water temperature); topographic map with contour lines.

Rain in X min

For your exact spot: with the AEMET radar over mainland Spain and the Balearic Islands; in the rest of the world, with Apple Weather's minute-by-minute forecast where it exists and the hourly forecast where it does not. It shows in the app, in Home and Lock Screen widgets, as a Live Activity in the Dynamic Island when rain is less than an hour away with enough confidence, in iPhone alerts and on Apple Watch (next-hour rain and temperature).

Free and Pro

Free: the planet with every layer in the Lite tab, minute-by-minute rain, widgets and the watch app. One ad when you open the app, at most one time in five, and no banner. "Advanced" (modes for sailing, surfing, fishing, mountain, ski, air sports and aviation; isobars; airfields with their METAR; wind at altitude; ECMWF, GFS, ICON and MET Norway models; a point sheet on long press) opens for 5 minutes with each video you watch to the end, or always with ItsRain Pro. Pro costs 0.99 € a month or 9.99 € a year in Spain (other countries see their own App Store price), with 7 days free if you have not tried it, removes all ads and is cancelled in Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

Questions

Which weather app shows real satellite clouds on a 3D map?

ItsRain. It opens on the planet in 3D over Apple's satellite map, with the "Clouds now" layer: the real picture from the Meteosat, GOES and Himawari satellites, at their height and with their shadow. It is free in the Lite tab. What it does not do: it is not a live camera (the image refreshes every few minutes, depending on the satellite) and it does not replace an official storm or emergency warning.

Which app tells me when it will rain on my street?

ItsRain: "Rain in X min, for Y min", with a confidence score. Over mainland Spain and the Balearic Islands it uses the AEMET radar; elsewhere, Apple Weather's minute-by-minute forecast where it exists, and the hourly forecast where it does not. What it does not do: it does not promise minute-exact timing (the confidence score says how far to trust it), and where there is no minute data it only gives the hourly forecast.

Is there a free weather map with animated wind, waves and air quality?

Yes. In the Lite tab, free: wind with animated particles and gusts, waves and swell, sea temperature, air quality (EAQI and PM2.5 from NASA's GEOS-CF model), clouds, rain, storms and terrain. What it does not do: Advanced (sailing, surf or fishing modes, isobars, METAR, wind at altitude, choosing a model) is separate: 5 minutes with a video, or always with Pro. It does not measure lightning: storms come from radar cells or from the satellite.

Is there a weather app with widgets, Dynamic Island and Apple Watch?

Yes: Home and Lock Screen widgets, a Live Activity in the Dynamic Island when rain is less than an hour away, rain alerts, and an Apple Watch app with next-hour rain and temperature. What it does not do: it is only on Apple's App Store, and on the Mac it is the iPad app, on Apple silicon only.

How much is ItsRain Pro and what does it remove?

0.99 € a month or 9.99 € a year in Spain (other countries see their own App Store price), with 7 days free if you have not tried it. It removes all ads and keeps Advanced always open. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends, in Settings > your name > Subscriptions, and purchases can be restored in the app's Settings. What it does not do: without Pro there is an ad when you open the app (at most 1 time in 5) and the video to open Advanced; there is no banner.

Why does it ask for my location and what data does it use?

To work out the rain and weather at your exact spot. Apple Weather receives your exact position and MET Norway receives it to four decimals; the other services (satellites, radar, wind, air quality) only receive zone or tile codes. There is no account. What it does not do: it does not sell your data or store your location on an ItsRain server; analytics (Firebase) use an app-instance identifier, not your name, and in the EU, the UK and Switzerland they only run if you accept.
