Privacy policy · Date: November 17, 2026

ItsRain · Help and support

Summary: ItsRain has no accounts and no sign-up. Your location is used on your phone to calculate the weather for your spot, and it is not stored on any ItsRain server. Analytics (Firebase) is switched on if you accept it in the EU, the UK and Switzerland; elsewhere it runs without asking. Its data is linked to an identifier of your app installation, not to your name. The free version shows Google AdMob ads; ItsRain Pro removes them. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Who is responsible

Gabriel Micó Victoria, developer of ItsRain. For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights, write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Location

ItsRain uses your location to work out the rain and the weather for your exact spot. The calculation is done on your phone. Your location is not stored on any ItsRain server.

For the radar, the phone asks the server for the radar images of your area, without sending your exact position. Apple Weather receives your exact position for the forecast. MET Norway receives your position to four decimal places (about 10 metres), also from Apple Watch. The radar image server, like any web server, sees the phone's IP address.

Accounts and sign-up

ItsRain does not ask for an account, email or registration.

Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics (Google) to learn how the app is used: which screens and features are used, how many ads are shown, and purchases (their value and currency). Events are linked to an identifier of your app installation that Firebase assigns. They are not fully anonymous: they do not contain your name, email or account, and we do not use Apple's IDFA or use them for tracking across other apps. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland it is switched on only if you accept Google's consent notice. Elsewhere it runs without asking. Events do not carry your location.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Before showing them, the app uses Google's consent notice (UMP) and, on iPhone, Apple's tracking prompt (ATT). You can change your choice in your phone's settings. ItsRain Pro removes all ads.

Purchases and subscriptions

ItsRain Pro is bought and managed through Apple (StoreKit). ItsRain does not see your card or payment details. You can cancel the subscription in Apple Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

Rain alerts

Rain alerts are local notifications on your own phone. They do not go through any ItsRain server.

Global map

The global map downloads images from NASA, EUMETSAT and Apple Maps. These services see the area you are looking at and the IP address, not your exact position.

Version check

To know whether there is a new version, the app queries Apple's App Store service (itunes.apple.com).

Your rights

You can ask us for information about your data, its correction or its deletion by writing to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, the date above will change with it.
