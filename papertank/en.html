Paper Tanks

Round-based strategy with paper tanks

Coming soon to the App Store · iPhone and iPad

Help & support · Privacy policy

Build a column of paper tanks, kit it out round by round and watch it fight on its own on a cardboard battlefield. Against computer-controlled generals or in online rooms of up to 10 players. No accounts.

Three modes

Solo

A 15-round run against three computer-controlled generals. Free, offline and with no account.

Find a rival

A 1-vs-1 duel or an open room of up to 10 real generals; in the room, after 30 seconds bots fill the empty seats.

With friends

Create a duel (2 seats) or a private or open room (2 to 10 seats), share its 6-character code and start whenever you want.

Both online modes are unlocked by the one-time "No Banner" purchase.

What is inside

  • 24 tanks from 6 armies (USA, United Kingdom, USSR, Germany, France and Japan), from fast scouts to super-heavies.
  • Gear that changes the model: cannons, tracks, engines, armour, systems and banners; radar, smoke screen, repair kit, mine layer and active defences that stop one projectile and then rearm.
  • Synergies by army, type and alliance, and an economy of loot, money and interest.
  • Rounds against paper critters: ants, beetles, spiders, wasps, mice and, at the end, the Cat.
  • A paper battlefield that folds and tanks that burst into paper shavings when they fall. 6 boards: forest, desert, snow, city, mud and a desk.
  • Paper styles: Kraft (free) and, as optional purchases that only change the look, Notebook, Newsprint and Blueprint.
  • Free, director and overhead cameras, follow-a-tank with a double tap, 1× or 2× speed and a tutorial in your first game.
  • In English and Spanish. For iPhone and iPad.

Online without an account

You do not sign up to play in a room: the game uses an anonymous session and gives you an invented name (adjective, noun and number) that you can change. There is no chat and no free text. Each player's plan stays hidden until everyone has finished preparing, and room data deletes itself shortly after the game ends. More in the privacy policy.

Price and ads

Paper Tanks is free. It shows a discreet banner on menu screens only, a full-screen ad when a game starts (while it is being prepared, with a notice first) and, once in a while, when you open the game. The one-time "No Banner" purchase removes the banner and unlocks the online modes; the other ads stay. Nothing paid gives an advantage in a game and there are no random boxes.

Quick questions

Do I need an account?

No. Neither to play solo nor to play online.

Does it work offline?

Solo mode does. The online modes need a connection.

Can I play online without other players?

Yes: any seat nobody takes is filled by a bot, so a whole game can be played alone.

Which devices?

iPhone and iPad (iOS), in English and Spanish.
