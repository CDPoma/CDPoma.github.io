About Paper Tanks · Privacy policy

Paper Tanks is a round-based strategy game with paper tanks: you equip your column in the workshop and the battle plays out on its own, in real time. You can play solo and offline, or online in rooms of 6 players (all against all) or in a duel against your friend (with the "No Banner" purchase). There are no accounts. Something not working? Write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

How to play

  1. You start with one tank. Every round you pick 1 of 3 loot items (a tank or a piece of gear) and spend your Budget (the paper clips you earn each round): more tanks at the Quartermaster, gear in each tank's slots, or save it to earn interest.
  2. Drag your tanks around the workshop plan (vanguard, centre, rear): each one performs best in its ideal spot. Tap a tank to see its sheet and fit gear to it: drag a piece onto the tank (or tap the piece, then the tank), check the preview, use AUTO-EQUIP to spread them with one tap and "Undo" if you change your mind. Every piece is visibly mounted on the tank.
  3. Press GO: your column rolls out onto the field, a countdown runs and the battle plays out on its own. You only move the camera.
  4. Win or lose you earn paper clips; if you lose, your general loses health. At zero you are out and keep watching as a spectator. It is 15 rounds; the last one standing wins or, after round 15, whoever has the most health left.
  5. Synergies (tanks of the same army, the same type or an alliance) give bonuses: see them in the left panel of the workshop.

The three modes

  • SOLO: a run against three computer-controlled generals. Free, offline.
  • FIND A RIVAL: choose a 1-vs-1 duel (if nobody shows up within about 30 seconds, you decide: keep waiting or play against a bot) or a room of 6 generals, all against all (after 30 seconds bots fill the empty seats).
  • WITH FRIENDS: create a room of 6 seats (all against all) or a duel against your friend and share its 6-character code with "Copy", "WhatsApp" or "Message"; your friends enter with "Join with a code". In the room you start whenever you want and bots take the empty seats.

Both online modes are unlocked by the one-time "No Banner" purchase.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. To play in a room, the game uses an anonymous session and gives you an invented name (you can change it with "New name"). There is no chat and no free text. More details in the privacy policy.

Can I play online without other players?

Yes. In "Find a rival" the room starts by itself after 30 seconds; in "With friends" you start whenever you want. Any seat nobody takes is filled by a bot, so you can play a whole game even if you are alone.

My friend cannot find my room

Check that they typed the 6 characters of the code correctly (it never contains "0", "O", "1" or "I"), that their game is the same version as yours and that the room is not full or already started. If it fails many times in a row, the game asks them to wait a few minutes.

It says "unavailable" or "no connection" when finding a rival

The online modes need internet (wifi or mobile data). If there is no connection, the game says so and lets you go back to the menu; Solo mode needs no network.

What happens if I leave a room mid-game?

From the pause menu, "Leave the room": a bot plays your column for the rest of the game and you cannot return to that room. If you lose connection, the same happens after about a minute. If your general falls, you carry on as a spectator: you can follow another player and watch their fights, also with the "On board" camera.

How do I remove the banner? How do I restore the purchase?

On the home screen, tap "No Banner": one-time purchase, no subscription. It removes the banner and unlocks Find a rival and With friends; the full-screen ad at the start of a game and the app-open ad stay. If you change device or reinstall, tap "Restore purchases" in that same window or in Settings, with the same Apple account.

I paid and the online mode or the banner is still locked

The payment is confirmed with the App Store and sometimes takes a few minutes. Tap "Restore purchases". If it stays the same, write to us with your device model and the date of the purchase.

What are paper styles?

They change the look of the whole game world (the tanks' paper, the board and the desk). Kraft is free; Notebook, Newsprint and Blueprint are optional purchases, separately or all together ("All styles"), with no random boxes and no advantage in a game. In "Paper styles" you can tap "Try" to see them live before buying.

When do ads appear?

A banner on menu screens (home, round result and end of the run; never in the workshop or during battles), a full-screen ad when a game starts, while it is being prepared and with a notice first (at most one every 90 seconds; never in the tutorial) and, on 1 of every 5 openings of the game, an app-open ad (never on the day of installation). They never appear in the middle of a battle.

The battle is too fast or too slow

Tap the speed button (1× or 2×) on the battle screen. In the pause menu you can also restart the run or quit to the menu.

How do I move the camera?

With one finger you drag over the ground; with two fingers you pinch to zoom, twist to rotate and drag up or down to tilt. Double-tap a tank to follow it. At the bottom right you have FREE, DIRECTOR (automatic cuts), OVERHEAD (view from above) and ON BOARD (first person, inside the tank).

Which languages does it have?

English and Spanish, following the device language.
