Privacy policy

About Paper Tanks · Help & support

Summary: Paper Tanks is a round-based strategy game with paper tanks. It has no accounts and asks for no personal data (no name, e-mail, phone, location or contacts). Solo mode works offline and sends nothing to any server of ours. The online modes (Find a rival and With friends) use an anonymous session and a name invented by the game; their data (the room, each round's plan and the result) is deleted automatically shortly after the game ends. Google ads are only personalised if you give your consent, and usage analytics (no advertising identifier) are only turned on if you accept Google's form where it is required.

Who is responsible

Gabriel Micó Victoria, developer of Paper Tanks. For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights, write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

No accounts or sign-up

Paper Tanks has no accounts, users, e-mails or passwords, and it does not ask for your name, phone, location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. On the device itself it only keeps local settings and progress: the volumes, the chosen paper style, whether you have done the tutorial, the invented name you use in rooms, a copy of the last purchase state confirmed by the store, the counter for the app-open ad, your consent decision and, if you have used an online mode, the anonymous session token. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

The online modes: anonymous session, invented name and rooms

  • Identity: the first time you enter an online mode, the game asks Supabase for an anonymous session (no e-mail or password) and keeps its token on your device to keep the same identifier. That identifier is not linked to any personal data.
  • Name: nobody types a name. The game builds one from word lists (adjective, noun and number) and you can ask for another with "New name". It is the only thing the other players in the room see. There is no chat, no messages and no user-generated content.
  • Rooms and duels: a room has up to 10 seats (a 1-vs-1 duel is a room of 2) and a 6-character code that you share with whoever you want. Empty seats are taken by bots, which are not people and send no data. Whoever leaves (or loses connection) is replaced by a bot.
  • Hidden plan: the plan you send each round cannot be seen by anyone until all players have sent theirs; the server does not simulate the game, it only stores the orders and the hashes so that every device can check it played the same game.
  • Automatic deletion: finished rooms are deleted by themselves about one hour after they end (three days if the game got out of sync); abandoned waiting rooms likewise; and the failed-code counter (a brake against code guessing) after one day. A scheduled clean-up runs every 5 minutes.
  • Asking for deletion: since there is no account, there is nothing to delete from the app, and room data is anonymous and expires on its own. If you want us to delete something sooner, write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com with the room code and the approximate time.

Ads

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on menu screens only (home, round result and end of the run; never in the workshop or during battles), a full-screen ad when a game starts, while it is being prepared, preceded by a notice (never during the game or the tutorial, and at most one every 90 seconds) and an app-open ad on 1 of every 5 openings of the game (at launch or when you return after 30 minutes or more away), which never shows on the day of installation.

Consent. Before requesting any ad, the game shows Google's consent form (where the law requires it: European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland) and, on iPhone and iPad, also asks for Apple's tracking permission (ATT). If you consent, Google may show personalised ads and use the device's advertising identifier (IDFA), together with technical data such as the IP address and device model, under Google's policy. If you do not, the ads shown will not be personalised; if there is no valid consent where it is mandatory, the game does not request ads. Google receives nothing about your games.

You can change your choice whenever you want: on iPhone and iPad, in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; and, in Europe, in Paper Tanks → Settings → "Ad privacy".

Purchases

The one-time "No Banner" purchase removes the banner and unlocks the online modes (Find a rival and With friends); it does not remove the full-screen ad at the start of a game or the app-open ad. The paper styles (Notebook, Newsprint and Blueprint, separately or all together) are optional purchases that only change the look of the game. They are one-time purchases, with no subscription and no random boxes, and none of them gives an advantage in a game. Payment is handled by the App Store with your Apple account; we never see payment data. RevenueCat checks the purchase with the store using an anonymous identifier of the installation, with no name or e-mail. There is "Restore purchases" in the "No Banner" window and in Settings. If you have given analytics consent, RevenueCat also receives the Google Analytics installation identifier so purchases show up in the same statistics.

Usage analytics

Paper Tanks uses Google Analytics for Firebase on iPhone and iPad to learn how many people play and how, so the game can be improved.

  • What is collected: automatic usage events (first open, session); six custom events with no personal data: carrera_inicio (a run starts), ronda_fin (round number, win/loss/draw and mode), carrera_fin (final place and rounds played), compra_sin_banner, tutorial_inicio and tutorial_fin; an identifier of the app installation, which is not your name, an e-mail or the advertising identifier; and basic technical data: approximate country, operating system and game version.
  • What is not collected: the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used, nor Apple's tracking permission, for analytics; no user identifier or personal properties are assigned, and a room code, your invented name or anything that identifies a person is never sent.
  • What for: usage statistics only (active users, versions, countries, how many runs are finished, how many people get past the tutorial). They are not linked to your name or to any account, nor used to follow you across other companies' apps or websites. For Apple, the installation identifier counts as a device identifier, which is why it appears that way in the App Store privacy label.
  • With whom: Google processes it as a data processor. The installation identifier is also shared with RevenueCat (see above).

Consent and control. At launch, analytics are off. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland they are only turned on if you accept storing data on your device in Google's consent form (the same one as for ads), and if you refuse no usage data is collected. Outside that area, where prior consent is not required, they are turned on without a form. You can change your choice in Settings → "Ad privacy" (where it appears) or ask for it to stop by writing to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Device permissions

  • Tracking (ATT): Apple's prompt for the advertising identifier, only for ads.
  • Paper Tanks does not use the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or notifications.

How long data is kept

  • Solo mode: nothing leaves the device.
  • Online rooms (Supabase): finished rooms, about one hour after they end (three days if there was a desync); failed-code counters, one day; the server's technical logs, according to Supabase's policy.
  • Local game data (settings, invented name, anonymous session token…): until you uninstall the game.
  • Analytics, advertising and purchases: according to the retention periods of Google (Analytics, AdMob) and RevenueCat.

Children

Paper Tanks is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from minors; there is no chat and no content from other users. If you think a minor has provided data, write to us at gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask at any time for access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability of your data (GDPR) by writing to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com. Since Paper Tanks has no accounts, almost everything is on your device, where you can delete it yourself by uninstalling the game. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or to your country's authority.

Legal basis: the data needed to play in a room is processed to provide the service you asked for (performance of the service); personalised ads and usage analytics, with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time; non-personalised ads, for the legitimate interest of funding the free version.

Security

All communications are encrypted (HTTPS). The room tables do not accept direct writes: they are only modified through server functions that validate every value (seats, codes, names, sizes), with limits on rooms per hour and on code attempts. Each device also validates the plans it receives from the others with the same game rules and discards anything invalid.
