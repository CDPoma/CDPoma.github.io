Privacy policy

Help and support

Summary: TAI is a virtual pet game for all ages. You play without signing up, and it never asks for a real name, email, phone number, location, photos or contacts; the account is optional and needs no email. Creatures are stored on our server (in the European Union) so that friends can look after them. Friends are added only with a code: there is no chat and no way to meet strangers. Google ads are only personalized if you give your consent. Purchases and links that leave the app sit behind a grown-up check. Everything can be deleted instantly from Settings.

Who is responsible

Gabriel Micó Victoria, developer of TAI. For any question about data, or to exercise your rights, write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Playing without signing up

The first time TAI opens, the app creates an anonymous identity on our server (Supabase): a random identifier, with no email, phone number or password. The session is stored on the device itself and is used to play.

Optional account. From Settings you can create an account with a username (3 to 20 letters, numbers or underscores) and a password (6 characters or more), to get the creatures back on another device or after deleting the app. No email is requested, so a forgotten password cannot be recovered. The password is stored by Supabase's sign-in system in hashed form (only its fingerprint, not the password itself); we cannot see it.

What is stored on the server

Everything is stored with Supabase, on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).

  • Identity: the random identifier and, if the optional account is created, the username and the hashed password. The sign-up date.
  • Player name: the one typed on the Friends screen (up to 24 characters; until one is typed, it shows “Someone”). Friends can see it. We recommend a nickname rather than a full name.
  • Friend code: 6 random letters and numbers, so other players can link up.
  • Creatures: the names given to them, their species, their state (hunger, happiness, hygiene, energy, weight, age, whether asleep), the world they live in and the list of previous creatures (History).
  • Friendships: who the player is linked with and since when.
  • Couples: proposals for two friends' creatures to become a couple, whether they were accepted and which one moved to the other's world.
  • Care: who fed, cleaned, played with or put each creature to bed, and when (so the game knows who has cared for it the most).
  • Tic-tac-toe games with friends: the board, the turns and who won.
  • Purchases: which creatures have been unlocked. No payment details.
  • Language and time zone of the device (for example, “Europe/Madrid”): so the creature sleeps at night in the player's country and notifications arrive in their language.
  • Notification token and device type (Android or iPhone), only if notifications are allowed: it is the address they are sent to.

On the device itself the app keeps the session, the chosen language, whether sound is on, the last creature opened and whether the game panels are hidden.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, audio or real names. Apart from the Google ads described below, we use no analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share data with anyone.

Friends: by code only, no chat

  • You can only become friends with someone by knowing their code. There is no search, no player list and no matchmaking with strangers. When a code is entered, both players become linked, and either of them can unlink at any time.
  • Friends see each other's player name and creatures with their state, and can look after them, challenge each other to tic-tac-toe and propose that two creatures become a couple (the other player accepts or declines).
  • If two creatures become a couple, one moves to the other's world. There, the host's friends can see it but not look after it, and they don't see its owner's name unless they are friends.
  • There is no chat and no messaging between players. The only things a player types that others can see are their player name and their creatures' names.
  • The creature's lines are written in advance inside the app: there is no artificial intelligence, and nothing that happens in the game is sent to an AI service.
  • When visiting a friend's creature that isn't well, there is a “Tell them on WhatsApp” button. It sits behind the grown-up question and only opens WhatsApp with a ready-made message; the adult decides whether to send it and to whom. TAI sends nothing and doesn't know who it was sent to.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the top and, sometimes, a full-screen ad right before a surprise (when a creature hatches, on a trip or on an evolution), at most one every 90 seconds and never when the app opens.

Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, before requesting any ad the app shows Google's consent form. On iPhone it also asks for Apple's tracking permission (ATT). If you consent, Google may show personalized ads and use the device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, advertising ID on Android), together with technical data such as the IP address and device model, as described in Google's policy. If you don't, ads still appear, but non-personalized. Google receives nothing from the game: no names, no creatures, no friends.

You can change your choice at any time: on iPhone, in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android, in the device's Google settings (Ads), where the advertising ID can be deleted or reset; and, in Europe, in TAI's Settings → “Ad privacy”.

Any purchase removes the ads forever.

In-app purchases

Creatures can be bought (a one-time purchase each). Before buying, the app asks a grown-up question (a multiplication). Payment is handled by Apple or Google with the store account; we never see payment details. RevenueCat checks the purchase with the store and tells us which creature was unlocked; for that it knows the player's random identifier and the state of their purchases. Settings has a “Restore purchases” button.

Notifications

If notifications are allowed, TAI sends one when a creature needs help: to the owner when it is missing something, to their friends if the owner doesn't show up, and to everyone if it is very weak. Never while it is asleep. The notification carries the creature's name (and, for friends, the owner's player name). They are sent with Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging and, on iPhone, through Apple's notification service. They can be turned off at any time in the device settings.

Deleting the account and all data

In Settings → “Delete my account and everything in it”, after the grown-up question and a confirmation. Deletion is immediate and permanent: it removes the identity (and the username and password, if any), the player name, the friend code, the creatures and their history, friendships, couples and proposals, games, recorded purchases, language, time zone and notification token.

  • Friends' creatures that were living in the player's world go back home.
  • The care given to friends' creatures still counts for those creatures, but with nothing that identifies the player.
  • Purchase receipts are kept by Apple or Google in the store account, under their own policies.

Until deleted, data is kept for as long as the account exists. If the app is uninstalled without deleting the account first, the data stays on the server: write to us with the friend code and we will delete it.

Minors and your rights

TAI is for all ages and children use it too. That is why it asks for no personal data, the account is optional and needs no email, there is no chat, friends are added only by code, and purchases and links out of the app sit behind a grown-up check. Any user, and for a minor their parents or guardians, can ask at any time to see, correct or delete their data, and exercise the other GDPR rights (access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability), by writing to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or your local authority.

Legal basis: data is processed to provide the game that was requested (performance of the service); personalized ads, on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time; and non-personalized ads, on a legitimate interest in funding the free version.

Security

All communication is encrypted (HTTPS). On the server, each player can only read their own data and what their friends show them, and the game rules are enforced by the server, not the device.
