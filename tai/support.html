Help and support

Privacy policy

TAI is a virtual pet game for all ages with shared care between friends: your friends can feed, clean and play with your creature when you're away, and they get a notification if it needs help. Something not working? Write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to sign up?

No. You can play as soon as the app opens, with no email or phone number. The account is optional (Settings → Your account): a username and a password to get your creatures back on another device or if you delete the app.

I forgot my password

TAI doesn't ask for an email, so there is no way to prove the account is yours in order to change it: the password cannot be recovered. That is why the app asks you to write it down somewhere safe when you create it.

How do I add a friend?

Your 6-character code is on the Friends screen. Share it with whoever you like (or enter theirs) and you'll be linked. Friends can only be added by code: there is no search and no strangers. You can unlink from a friend at any time from that same screen.

What happens if I don't look after my creature?

Its bars (hunger, happiness, hygiene and energy) go down in real time, even with the app closed, but not while it sleeps at night. If it becomes very weak, you and your friends have one hour to help it; if nobody does, it is lost and moves to History.

I bought a creature and it doesn't show up

Payment is confirmed with the store and sometimes takes a few minutes. If it still doesn't show up, go to Settings → Restore purchases. Any purchase removes the ads forever.

Why does it ask me a multiplication?

It's the grown-up question: it appears before buying, before opening anything outside the app (WhatsApp, this website) and before deleting the account, so a young child can't do it by accident.

I'm not getting notifications

Check that notifications for TAI are allowed in your device settings. No notifications are sent while the creature is asleep.

How do I delete my account and all my data?

In Settings → “Delete my account and everything in it”. Everything is deleted instantly and it can't be undone. If you no longer have the app, write to us with your friend code and we'll delete it. More details in the privacy policy.

Contact

Questions, bugs or suggestions: gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com. If it's about a creature or a purchase, include your friend code (it's on the Friends screen) and your device model.
