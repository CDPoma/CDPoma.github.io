Support and privacy

Delete my account

Quick summary: BikeFuel detects when you have crashed on your bike and alerts your guardians with your last known position. To do that it needs your location while you ride, including when the phone is locked. That information is used for that and nothing else, only the people you accept as guardians can see it, and you can delete all of it whenever you want. No audio is recorded and no data is sold.

What the app does

BikeFuel is a safety app for road and mountain cyclists, with two modes:

  • Ride mode: you are on the bike. The app watches your ride and, if it detects a crash, gives you a few seconds to cancel the alert by holding «I'm OK» for 1.5 seconds (or by saying «estoy bien»). If you don't cancel, it alerts your guardians with your last known position. Detection thresholds are different for road and for mountain riding.
  • Guardian mode: you get the alert if someone in your circle crashes. It is the default state of every installation, and you can use the app only as a guardian without ever riding a bike.

Detection relies on the accelerometer, the gyroscope, GPS, the microphone's sound level and, if you have them, the Bluetooth sensors on your bike.

Who can see your location

  • Your routes are private. Routes and their points can only be read by their owner, that is, you.
  • A guardian receives your location only inside an alert, or live if you turn on live mode and authorise that particular guardian.
  • Nobody joins your circle without your permission. If someone enters your 8-character code, a request is created that you accept or reject. Until you accept, that person sees nothing of yours.

When there is no data connection

Losing coverage is common on a bike, so there is a fallback alert:

  • Android: the app sends an SMS to your guardians from your own phone.
  • iPhone: Apple does not allow an app to send an SMS without the user tapping it, so the fallback is a Bluetooth alert to nearby phones that also run BikeFuel. It is a signed relay that forwards the alert; it never carries personal content.

What we do NOT do

  • Your location is never sold, and never handed to advertisers or insurers. It leaves your account towards a third party in two cases, both so that a place can be shown: to turn coordinates into a street name when there is an alert (OpenStreetMap) and to draw the satellite map of the live view (Esri). Both receive only the position, never your name. They are in the table below.
  • No audio is recorded. The microphone measures the noise level in the moment and nothing else. Your voice is not recorded during the countdown either.
  • Your position is not tracked when you are not on a ride. Tracking starts when you begin Ride mode and stops when you end it.
  • Your position is shared with nobody except the guardians you have accepted, and only during an alert or in the live mode you turn on.

Free and Pro

Crash detection and alerting your guardians are free, always. Pro is a one-time purchase that removes ads and adds live route sharing, ride history and Bluetooth sensors. Whether you are Pro is checked by RevenueCat against the store; it does not depend on anything that can be changed on the phone.

Permissions

  • Location, always — needed in the background because on a ride your phone is locked or in your jersey pocket, which is exactly when it has to know where you are. Without it, detection cannot work with the screen off.
  • Motion (accelerometer and gyroscope) — to detect the crash.
  • Microphone — to measure the noise level of an impact. Nothing is recorded.
  • Speech recognition — only during the countdown, to cancel the alert by saying «estoy bien».
  • Bluetooth — for cycling sensors and, on iPhone, the fallback alert to nearby phones.
  • Advertising identifier (ATT, iPhone only) — for personalised ads in the free version. You can say no.
  • Send SMS (Android only) — for the fallback alert when there is no data.
  • Notifications — for the alert countdown and for alerts about your riders.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

Your routes and alerts are kept while your account is open. You can delete a bike from the app, and its rides and history go with it. To delete the whole account and everything linked to it there is a dedicated page that also works without the app installed: how to delete my account.

Children's privacy

BikeFuel is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has signed up, write to us and we will delete the account.

Important notice. BikeFuel is an aid, not a certified emergency system. Crash detection can fail, both by missing a real crash and by firing without reason, and the alert depends on there being coverage or another phone with the app nearby. It does not replace calling emergency services (112 in Europe). Do not rely on it as your only means of help.
