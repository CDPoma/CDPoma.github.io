Support and privacy

You can delete your BikeFuel account and all your data whenever you want, with or without the app installed. There are two ways. The first is instant; the second is for people who no longer have the app.

Option 1 · Inside the app (instant)

  1. Open BikeFuel and go to Tu cuenta (Your account).
  2. Tap Eliminar mi cuenta (Delete my account) and confirm.

It is deleted immediately and you don't need to write to anyone.

Option 2 · Without the app installed

Send an email to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com from the same address you registered with, with the subject:

Borrar mi cuenta de BikeFuel

That is how we check the account is yours. We will reply and delete your account within 30 days at the most; in practice, much sooner.

What is deleted

Everything, with nothing left half-done:

  • Your account and profile (email, name and phone number).
  • Your bikes.
  • Your routes and their tracks, with all their points.
  • Sensor readings.
  • Your alerts.
  • The notification tokens of your phones.
  • Guardian links in both directions: the riders you were watching over and the guardians who were watching over you.

Your guardians stop seeing you in their circle.

What is NOT deleted: the Pro purchase. Pro lives in your App Store or Google Play account, not in your BikeFuel account. Deleting the account does not cancel or refund the purchase. For that you have to go to the store where you bought it (App Store or Google Play) and manage it there.

This cannot be undone. Once the account is deleted nothing can be recovered: not your routes, not your history, not your guardian circles. If you want to use BikeFuel again later, you will have to create a new account from scratch.

Questions

If anything is unclear, write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com. Details of what data is collected are in Support and privacy.
