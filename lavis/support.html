How to use each tool

The tool names are the ones shown in the app.

ToolHow to use it
BrushesBrushes bar. The 8 basic brushes are free; the six Pro brushes (Pastel, Chalk, Dry brush, Mist, Fine pen and Stamp) are under “Pro brushes”. Without Pro, you get “Watch an ad and use Pro brushes for 20 min” or “Go Pro”.
LayersLayers bar. “Add layer” creates one. The free version has up to 3; for the fourth, the app asks for “Unlimited layers”. Each layer has “Show layer” or “Hide layer”, “Duplicate layer”, “Delete layer”, “Merge down”, “Move layer up” and “Move layer down”, “Opacity” and “Blend mode”.
Blend modesOn the layer, “Blend mode”. “Normal” is free. The others (Multiply, Screen, Overlay, Lighten, Darken, Add, Color and Luminosity) are Pro.
Undo and redo“Undo” and “Redo” in the bar. Free.
ColorColor bar: “Color wheel”, “Recent” and “Palette”. Free.
EyedropperEyedropper tool: takes the color from any point of the drawing. Free.
TransformTransform tool: “Scale”, “Rotate”, “Flip horizontally” and “Flip vertically”. “Apply” makes it one action you can undo; “Cancel” discards it. Free.
SymmetrySymmetry bar (Pro): “Off”, “Vertical mirror”, “Horizontal mirror” or “Four quadrants”.
Perfect ShapePerfect Shape tool (Pro): hold the pen still at the end of a stroke and it becomes a line, ellipse or rectangle.
Save and open .ora“Save” and “Open” in the bar. The .ora keeps the layers. Free.
Export PNG“Export PNG” in the bar. Free.
Export PSD“Export PSD” in the bar (Pro). It is saved with its layers.
See the whole drawing“Fit to screen” in the bar.

Pro, ads and unlocking

CaseWhat happens
On iPhone and iPadWhen you tap something Pro without access, for example “Watch an ad and use Symmetry for 20 min” appears, with “Go Pro” as the alternative. If you watch the ad to the end, that tool opens for 20 minutes. For more time, “Add 20 min more” (or “Watch an ad: 20 more min”, depending on the window) adds another 20 minutes to the same tool. If no ad is available at that moment, the app says “No ad available now. Try again in a moment.”
On Mac and WindowsWhen you tap something Pro, “Unlock with your phone (QR)” appears. The computer shows a code: in Lavis on your iPhone, tap “Unlock the computer” and scan it. Watch the full ad on the phone with “Watch ad”; in a few seconds the tool opens on the computer. Each code expires after 10 minutes: if it does, ask for “Get another code”.
Pro“Go Pro” opens the subscription purchase: €1.99 a month or €14.99 a year in Spain. When you install it you get all of Pro for 7 days, no card, once. At the end, “Keep going with the free tools” or “Keep going free, with ads” (on iPhone and iPad).

Ads only appear in the free version on iPhone and iPad. Mac and Windows have no ads. Pro removes all of them.

Restore purchases

If you already bought Pro and it does not show as active, open the Pro window and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same account you bought with: your Apple account on iPhone, iPad and Mac, or your Microsoft account on Windows.

  • If it is recovered: “Purchase restored: Pro is on.”
  • If there is no active subscription: “We didn’t find any active subscription to restore.”
  • If the connection fails: “Couldn’t restore. Check your connection and try again.”

Cancel the subscription

Pro is a subscription that renews automatically, at the same price, until you cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

  • iPhone, iPad and Mac: in the Pro window, “Manage subscription”, or at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Windows: at account.microsoft.com/services.

Deleting Lavis does not cancel the subscription: cancel it in the store.

Your drawings

Drawings are saved on your device or wherever you choose when exporting. Lavis has no accounts and no cloud copy of its own. Before you uninstall the app, save what you want to keep with “Save”, “Export PNG” or “Export PSD” (Pro). More details in the privacy policy.
