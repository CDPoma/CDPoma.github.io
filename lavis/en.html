What Lavis is

Lavis is a drawing and painting app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows. You paint with brushes that respond to pen pressure and tilt, work with layers, make selections and text, undo and redo, and export to PNG, to .ora (with its layers) or, with Pro, to PSD.

For free you can paint with the 8 basic brushes, use up to 3 layers, make selections, smudge, add text and export PNG. Lavis Pro, €1.99 a month or €14.99 a year in Spain, adds unlimited layers, blend modes, Perfect Shape, symmetry, PSD export, the six Pro brushes, Liquify, process video export and the brush editor. When you install it, you get all of Pro free for 7 days, no card needed.

How to unlock a Pro tool without paying

On iPhone and iPad (free)

When you tap a Pro tool, you can watch a rewarded ad. If you watch it to the end, that tool opens for 20 minutes. Times add up, and they only apply to the tool you tapped.

On Mac and Windows (free)

The computer shows a QR code. Scan it with Lavis on your iPhone, watch the full ad there, and that tool opens for 20 minutes on the computer. You need the iPhone with Lavis to scan.

Platforms

iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows. Pro purchases are made in each store: Apple for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and Microsoft Store for Windows. Lavis has no accounts of its own.

Your drawings

Your drawings are not uploaded to any Lavis server. They are saved on your device or wherever you choose. The free version on iPhone and iPad shows ads from an outside provider; the details are in the privacy policy.

What it does not do

  • It adds no watermark and no time limit on drawing.
  • It shows no ads on Mac or Windows. On iPhone and iPad, only in the free version.
  • It has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It is not in the stores yet.

Frequently asked questions

Is Lavis free?

Yes. Free: painting with the 8 basic brushes, up to 3 layers, selection, smudge, text, undo and redo, color, eyedropper, transform the layer, saving and opening .ora, and exporting PNG. No watermark and no time limit on drawing.

What does Lavis Pro include?

Unlimited layers (from the fourth on), blend modes, Perfect Shape, symmetry, PSD export, the six Pro brushes, Liquify, process video export and the brush editor. Pro costs €1.99 a month or €14.99 a year in Spain, and when you install it you get all of Pro free for 7 days, no card needed.

How do I use a Pro tool without subscribing?

On iPhone and iPad, by watching a rewarded ad to the end: that tool opens for 20 minutes, and the time adds up. On Mac and Windows, scan the QR code shown on the computer with the iPhone app: you watch the full ad on the phone and the tool opens for 20 minutes on the computer.

Does Lavis add a watermark or a time limit?

No. Your drawings have no watermark, and there is no time limit on drawing.

Which file formats does it save and export?

It saves and opens .ora, which keeps the layers, and exports PNG. Exporting PSD, with its layers, is Pro.

Do my drawings leave my device?

No. Drawings are not uploaded to any Lavis server: they are saved on your device or wherever you choose, with the system save panel or the share sheet on iPhone and iPad.

Are there ads on Mac and Windows?

No. Ads only appear in the free version on iPhone and iPad. Pro removes all of them.

Where can I get Lavis?

It is not in the stores yet. It will be on the App Store (iPhone, iPad and Mac) and on Microsoft Store (Windows). This page will be updated when it has a link.
