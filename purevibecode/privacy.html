PureVibeCode

Privacy policy · Last updated: 16/11/2026

Help and support

Summary: PureVibeCode has no accounts of its own. Your projects, your conversations and your AI keys stay on your computer (Mac or Windows). What you type in the chat and the project files the AI needs are sent to the AI you choose, with your own account or key, and only after you agree. On iPhone and iPad, the free version shows ads from Google AdMob; Pro removes them. The phone is a remote control for your computer: it does not generate code and does not store AI keys.

Who is responsible

Gabriel Micó Victoria, developer of PureVibeCode. For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights, write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

What PureVibeCode is

An app to create projects by talking to an artificial intelligence. It has two parts: the desktop program for Mac and Windows, where the project lives and where the AI is connected, and the phone app for iPhone and iPad, which shows and controls that computer remotely.

No accounts of its own

PureVibeCode does not ask for registration, email or a sign-in of its own. There are no user profiles on any PureVibeCode server. Each device has only an anonymous technical identifier so it can connect (see section 6).

1. On your computer

Projects, their files, conversations, settings and the preview are stored on your computer. PureVibeCode does not receive that content. When you build, record or take a screenshot, the result also stays on your computer, unless you upload it yourself to a service (section 2).

2. Your keys and accounts

API keys and sign-ins for AI services (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, GitHub Copilot or others you add), and the keys for stores or GitHub that you connect, are stored in your computer's system keychain (the macOS Keychain or the Windows Credential Manager). They are not in the project database, they do not pass through the phone and they do not pass through any PureVibeCode server.

If you connect services you choose, your computer talks to them directly, with your credentials:

  • GitHub: to upload the project and, if you ask, to build it in the cloud with GitHub Actions in your own repository. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • App Store Connect (Apple) and Google Play Console: to upload your apps and check their status with the keys of your developer account.
  • Microsoft Store: for purchasing Pro on Windows.

3. The AI you choose

To answer you, the AI you have connected receives what you type in the chat, the images you attach and the project files it needs to read or change. It is sent from your computer directly to that company, with your account or key. That company handles the data under its own terms:

  • Anthropic (Claude): anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • OpenAI: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy
  • GitHub Copilot: docs.github.com (privacy statement)

Your consent: the first time you send a message, the app asks you to accept this sending. Without your acceptance, nothing is sent to any AI. You can withdraw it in Settings; from then on nothing more is sent.

4. Ads (iPhone and iPad only, free version)

In the free version on iPhone and iPad, PureVibeCode shows ads from Google AdMob:

  • Banner: always, while you do not have Pro.
  • Rewarded ad: when you ask for it, to unlock 20 minutes of use of one project on the computer. Each project has its own time, and time is added only if Google confirms that you watched the whole ad.

Before requesting ads, the app shows Google's consent form (UMP) when the law in your region requires it. On iPhone and iPad it also asks for the system tracking permission (ATT). If you allow it, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier for more relevant ads; if you do not, you will see non-personalised ads. Where the law requires it (European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland), Settings offers «Ad privacy» so you can change your choice. AdMob handles the data it collects under its privacy policy.

With Pro, or during the 7-day free trial, there are no ads. On the computer there are no ads of our own or from third parties.

5. Unlocking with an ad

To unlock time on the computer with an ad, the computer requests an unlock from a PureVibeCode server hosted on Supabase (see section 6). The following is stored:

  • a hash (cryptographic fingerprint) of the computer identifier, not the identifier in clear;
  • the technical name of the project and whether the request is to open it or to create it;
  • the times of the request, its expiry and the unlock;
  • the confirmation Google sends when you have watched the whole ad: its transaction identifier, the request it corresponds to and whether it was used to add time.

No names, emails or IP addresses are stored. The server code does not record the IP address, headers or browser. Deletion periods: requests are deleted one day after they expire (they expire after 10 minutes); unlock times, one day after they end; Google's ad confirmations, after 7 days.

6. Connection between your phone and your computer

The phone and the computer talk through a PureVibeCode connection service hosted on Supabase, with its database in the European Union (Frankfurt). That service only relays messages between the two and does not store their content. As an exception, large screenshots or images that travel between the two are uploaded to private storage only so that the other device can download them; once downloaded, they are deleted.

To pair and recognise your devices, the service stores:

  • one anonymous Supabase session per device, with no email, no name and no password;
  • the name you give the computer and the name of the paired phone;
  • pairing codes only as a fingerprint (SHA-256), never in clear: they expire after 5 minutes and work once.

You can unpair the phone at any time. Its access is then revoked and the computer stops serving it.

7. iPhone and iPad

  • Camera: only to read the QR code the computer shows when pairing. The image is not stored or sent.
  • Pairing token: stored in the device's secure storage. There are no AI keys on the phone.
  • Preferences: language and theme, stored on the device.
  • Version check: when the app opens, it asks the App Store for the latest published version, to warn you if yours no longer works. Only the app identifier is checked.

8. Purchases and subscription

PureVibeCode Pro (1.99 € per month or 14.99 € per year, with 7 days free at the start) is paid through the Apple App Store on iPhone and iPad, and through the Microsoft Store on Windows. Each store handles the payment. To know whether you have Pro, PureVibeCode uses RevenueCat (revenuecat.com/privacy): RevenueCat receives an anonymous purchase identifier generated by the app and the subscription status, and does not receive the content of your projects. The computer checks with RevenueCat, using that same identifier, whether your phone's subscription is active.

9. Anonymous usage statistics

On iPhone and iPad the app uses Firebase Analytics (Google) only for the automatic usage events: first open, session start and the screens viewed. They are not linked to your identity, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used, and they are not used for advertising. They start switched off: in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland they are switched on only if you accept in the consent form; in the rest of the world, from the first launch.

On Mac and Windows anonymous usage statistics are switched off by default. If you turn them on in Settings, the program sends events to Google Analytics 4 (Measurement Protocol) such as opening the app, creating or opening a project, sending a message in the chat, which AI engine you use, building, recording a video, taking a screenshot, uploading to GitHub, requesting and completing an ad to unlock time, and Pro purchases with their amount and currency. No contents of chats, projects, files or paths are sent. The installation identifier is a random number that is deleted if you turn the statistics off; turning them on again creates a new one.

What we do not do

  • We do not use your projects, chats or keys for advertising.
  • We do not track your activity in other apps or on third-party websites.
  • We do not sell or transfer your data to anyone.
  • We do not create user profiles.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion or restriction of the data PureVibeCode processes, and object to certain processing, by writing to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com. The data on your computer or phone is under your control: you can delete projects, chats, keys and the pairing from the app itself, or uninstall it. For what the AI, the store or Google hold, ask that company. If you believe we do not handle your data properly, you can complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Children

PureVibeCode is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
