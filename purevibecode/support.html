Help and support

Privacy policy

PureVibeCode lets you create projects by talking to an artificial intelligence. The work happens on your computer (Mac or Windows); your iPhone or iPad controls it remotely. Something not working, or a question? Write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need?

  • A Mac or Windows computer with PureVibeCode installed, switched on and connected to the internet.
  • An account or key for the AI you want to use (Anthropic/Claude, OpenAI, Google or GitHub Copilot). PureVibeCode does not include any AI: you use yours.
  • For remote control from the phone: an iPhone or iPad with the PureVibeCode app.

How do I pair the phone with the computer?

On the computer, go to the «Phone» section and press «Pair a phone»: a QR code and a code to type appear. On your iPhone or iPad, open PureVibeCode and scan the QR code (the camera is only used to read that code) or type the code. Codes expire after 5 minutes: if one expires, generate another on the computer. You can unlink a phone at any time from the «Paired devices» list on the computer.

Why don't I see my projects on the phone?

The phone only shows projects when it is paired with a computer that is switched on and connected to the internet. Check both, and that the pairing is still active.

How does Pro work?

The app is free to download and has a free version with ads. PureVibeCode Pro costs 1.99 € per month or 14.99 € per year, with 7 days free at the start, and removes all ads and the timer. It is paid through the App Store on iPhone or iPad, and through the Microsoft Store on Windows. The phone's subscription also covers the computers paired with it.

To cancel or change it on iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. If you changed phone or account, use «Restore purchases» on the Pro screen.

Why do I see ads?

In the free version on iPhone and iPad a banner is shown. To use a project on the computer without Pro, you watch a rewarded ad and 20 minutes of that project are unlocked; each project has its own time. If the ad cannot be shown (no connection, no ad available), nothing opens: try again later or get Pro. Where the law requires it, you can change your choice under «Ad privacy» in Settings.

What data is sent to the AI?

Only what you type in the chat, the images you attach and the project files the AI needs. The first time, the app asks you to accept that sending; without your acceptance nothing is sent. AI keys are stored in your computer's keychain. Full details in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Projects, chats and keys are on your computer: delete them from the app or uninstall it. To remove the phone's access, unpair it from the computer. For what each AI or store holds, ask that company.

Can I use it without buying Pro?

Yes. The free version includes every feature, with ads. Without Pro, each rewarded ad you watch from the phone opens 20 minutes of one project on the computer; opening or creating another project asks for another ad.
