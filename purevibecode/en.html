What PureVibeCode is

PureVibeCode is an editor with chat, live preview and a terminal for building apps by chatting with your own AI. The AI writes the project files, and you see every change and decide every step. It runs on the Mac or the PC, and your iPhone or iPad is the remote control for the computer. No programming knowledge needed.

Run it from your phone

You pair your iPhone or iPad by scanning with its camera the QR code shown by the program on your computer. From then on that device is the keyboard and screen of the computer: you see your projects, write to the AI, check the preview and approve what it asks for.

The AI works on your computer, with the same projects, the same chat and the same permissions as if you were sitting in front of it. It does not ask you to confirm extra things because you are on another device: what it does ask, you answer there, in the same chat. Everything stays in one place: projects, conversations and preview.

The iPhone and iPad do not generate code: they are the remote control for your computer. The computer must be turned on and connected to the internet. The Lite version for Mac also pairs with the iPhone or iPad.

  • See your projects, the conversation with the AI and the latest preview capture.
  • Approve, in the same chat, the permissions the computer's AI asks for.
  • Keep everything in one place: projects, conversations and preview.

Your AI, your accounts

PureVibeCode does not include any AI: you use your own. You can use your account or your API key from the main AI providers, an AI that runs on your own computer, or any service compatible with the usual API. With an AI subscription, you use its official agent, signed in with its own account.

Keys are kept in your computer's keychain. Neither the iPhone nor the iPad stores AI keys.

You decide

The first time you send a message, the app asks your permission to send it to the AI you chose. Without your yes, nothing is sent. PureVibeCode also asks your permission to install tools, to upload the app to the store, and for new commands. You can choose permission modes that set how much the AI can do without asking you.

Free and Pro

  • Free: download and use at no cost. On iPhone and iPad there is a banner ad. On the computer, the Pro tools (terminal, official agents, local build, simulators, recording videos, work browser, store and creative tools) open for 20 minutes per project when you watch an ad on your iPhone or iPad with the QR code, or with Pro.
  • Pro: removes all ads and the time limit. In the US it costs $1.99 a month or $12.99 a year; other countries see their local price in the store.
  • Trial: 7 days free only when you buy on iPhone or iPad, once per account. On Windows there is no trial.
  • Pro on your iPhone or iPad also unlocks the paired computer.

Versions and platforms

  • iPhone and iPad: the remote control for the computer, on the App Store.
  • Mac, Lite version: free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. Editor, chat, preview and projects. macOS 12 or later.
  • Mac and Windows, full version: free, with the Pro tools unlocked by an ad or with Pro. Mac with macOS 12 or later; 64-bit PC with Windows 10 or later.

Your information

PureVibeCode has no accounts of its own. Your project files are sent only to the AI you choose. AI keys are kept on the computer, and neither the iPhone nor the iPad keeps them.
