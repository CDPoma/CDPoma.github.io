GoldSpot · Privacy policy

Safety and the law: GoldSpot is a helper, not a guarantee: it does not ensure that there is gold. Prospecting for gold is regulated; find out which permits you need and respect the law, private property and protected areas. Abandoned mines are dangerous (collapses, gases, water): do not enter.

GoldSpot shows on a world map where placer gold is most likely to be, with an explained score, together with known gold mines; it works out the score of any point on the map, identifies a mineral from a photo, and lets you save your places and post spots and nuggets with photos, anonymously if you like. Something not working, or have an idea? Write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

What is a spot?

A spot is a zone on the map (a point with a radius) where, according to the calculation, placer gold, the kind found in streams and rivers, is more likely. It can be scientific (the calculation says so, using geology, terrain and stream networks), prospectors' (clues such as old workings, place names or official panning areas) or both. Separately, “My spots” are the places you save yourself.

What does the score mean? Is it an estimate?

Yes, it is an indicative estimate. The score goes from 0 to 100 and is relative: it compares places with each other and is not a probability. Each spot's page shows the confidence (high, medium or low) and “Why it appears”, with the factors that raise or lower it: source rock upstream, distance to a stream, size of the catchment, valley floor, old terraces, breaks of slope, confluences, alluvial deposits, where the range ends, nearby gold mines and gold in sediments. The map has a resolution of about 460 m: a zone shows where to look, not where to dig, and a small stream or a bend is not visible.

Does GoldSpot guarantee that I will find gold?

No. Neither the score nor the identification of a mineral ensures that there is gold. A nearby mine raises the score, but the mine may be exhausted or may be for another mineral. Take it as a clue to plan with, not as a promise.

Can I prospect for gold anywhere? What laws apply?

No. Prospecting for gold is regulated and varies by country and region: in many places you need a permit or a licence, the land may be privately owned or under a mining claim, and in nature parks, other protected areas and some watercourses it may be forbidden. GoldSpot does not give permission for anything and does not check protected areas for you (“Analyse this point” warns “Protected areas (P2) not checked”). Find out before you go, respect the law and the permits of each place, and do not enter abandoned mines.

What is “Analyse this point”?

Press and hold the map anywhere, or open the round button, tap “Analyse this point”, move the map to the place and choose “Analyse here” (or “My location” if you are there), and GoldSpot works out on your phone the score of that point using the terrain around it, with the factors that explain it. It is calculated on your phone and is free. Without a connection it gives a partial score with low confidence. A point can give a different result from a nearby spot in the database, because it is calculated at a different resolution.

How do I identify a mineral?

On the Identify tab, take a photo of the stone, the nugget or what is left in your pan (close up, in natural light and with a coin next to it for scale) or pick one from your library. The AI tells you what mineral it looks like, what signs are visible, whether it could be gold (as opposed to pyrite, chalcopyrite or golden mica) and which safe home tests can confirm it. It never promises gold: when in doubt, it says “doubtful”. There is a limit of 40 identifications per day per account and an internet connection is required.

What is the video I see before identifying?

The free version shows a short full-screen Google AdMob video before each analysis of a mineral, and a banner at the bottom. Without watching all of the video, the photo is not analysed. With GoldSpot Pro the banner and the videos go away. If no video is available (for example without a connection, or without the consent Google asks for in Europe), you can try again, open “Ad privacy options” or go Pro.

How do I remove the ads? What is GoldSpot Pro?

GoldSpot Pro is a monthly or yearly App Store subscription that removes the banner and the videos; you turn it on from Settings. Every feature is also in the free version, and the limit of 40 identifications per day per account is the same with or without Pro. The price is shown in the app before you pay, with a 7-day free trial when the App Store offers it. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I bought Pro and it does not show up

Go to Settings → GoldSpot Pro → Restore purchases. Sometimes the store takes a few minutes to confirm the payment. If it still does not appear, write to us with the Apple receipt.

How do I cancel the Pro subscription?

It is managed from your Apple account: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → GoldSpot Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends so it does not renew.

Do I have to create an account?

To save spots, identify, post, add friends, report, hide or block, yes. The account does not ask for an email or a password: it is an anonymous session and only has a display name you choose. If you delete the app, you start from scratch. You can see the map, the spots, the mines and the finds without an account.

How do I save a place of my own?

With the round button on the map, tap “Save here” to save where you are using GPS. On Profile → My spots you also have “Save where I am” and “Add on the map”, to mark the place yourself. And you can tick “Save this place to My spots” when you post a find. Give it a name and, if you like, a note and up to 8 photos. Your spots can also be seen offline.

Are my spots private? How do I share them?

Yes: a spot is yours alone until you decide to share it. On its page, “Share” lets you choose between “With a friend” (they will see it is yours), “On my wall” (anyone can see it, under your name or as anonymous) and “Via WhatsApp or another app”. You can stop sharing it whenever you like.

How do I post a spot or a nugget anonymously?

After identifying a mineral, tap “Post this find”; for a spot of your own, “Share” → “On my wall”. “Post anonymously” is on by default: without your name, with the day instead of the time, with the photos stripped of metadata (EXIF and GPS) and with the location approximate (≈1 km) if you leave it that way. Keep in mind that the developer, who runs the server, can see which account posted each item; anonymity holds against other users. See the privacy policy for the limits.

How do I hide a post?

On its page, open the “More options” menu. On an anonymous post you will see “Hide this”: you stop seeing only that item (an anonymous author cannot be blocked because nobody knows who it is). If the author is visible, you can “Block” that person and stop seeing their posts. In both cases you can “Report”: a post with 3 reports is hidden automatically and the developer reviews it.

How do I delete a post, a spot or a photo?

Open its page and delete it. A find is removed together with its photos and its location. A photo of yours is deleted from the gallery.

How do I delete my account?

In Settings → Delete my account (on the Profile tab). Your profile, finds, spots and photos, friendships, reports, blocks and the copy of your spots kept on your phone are deleted. It cannot be undone.

Why does it ask for my location?

Only when you use it: to center the map on your position, for “My location” in “Analyse this point”, to save a place with GPS, to post a find with its location and for the “Go” screen. Your position is not sent on its own: only when you save a spot or post with a location. If you do not grant permission, you can mark the place by hand on the map or post without a location, after confirming.

What layers does the map have?

The background is Apple's satellite imagery, in 3D. With the round button at the bottom right you switch layers on or off: Towns and roads, Trails and tracks, Streams and springs and Contour lines, and GoldSpot's own: official topographic map, heat map, geology, stream network and markers (mines, scientific and prospectors' spots, your spots and finds). With the crosshair you read the elevation, the slope and the aspect of a point. The data is approximate: do not use it as your only guide in the mountains.

How do I get to a spot?

Open the spot or the find and tap “Go”: you will see the map with a line to the destination and, below it, the compass with the bearing and the distance. Tap “Turn on compass” if needed. “Directions by car” opens your maps app: Apple Maps on iPhone and iPad, the system maps app on Android and Google Maps on the web. If the location of a find is approximate, it takes you to the centre of the ≈1 km zone.

Does it work offline?

Spots, mines, the search and your saved spots can be browsed offline. A connection is needed for the map and satellite imagery, the terrain used by “Analyse this point” (without it, the analysis is partial), identifying minerals, posting and uploading photos.
