GymFuel

Your gym routine, calculated for you

GymFuel builds your routine: you choose which muscles to train and how hard, and the app works out the exercises, sets and weight for you, based on your maximums and what you have already trained. It includes a diagram for every exercise and Fuel points. There are no accounts or sign-up: your routines and history are stored on your phone.

With GymFuel Pro (subscription of 1.99 €/month or 14.99 €/year) you get long plans with a calendar, equipment selection, Hardcore intensity, full history, Partners, Apple Watch (on iPhone) and no ads.
