Privacy policy · Last updated: November 22, 2026

Help and support

Summary: SimOs has no accounts of its own and does not ask for or keep personal data. Your projects, screenshots, videos and keys stay on your PC (the keys, in Windows Credential Manager). What leaves the PC is little and is explained here: the check of the free 7-day trial with the computer's fingerprint, anonymous Google Analytics statistics only if you allow them, the downloads of the tools SimOs needs, and the services you choose to link (GitHub, Codemagic, App Store Connect). SimOs shows no ads.

Who is responsible

Gabriel Micó Victoria, developer of SimOs. For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights, write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

What SimOs is

A phone, tablet and desktop simulator for Windows 10 and 11: you open your web or hybrid app and see it on each device at app store sizes, take screenshots and videos and, with Pro, build it for mobile. Everything happens on your PC; it only connects to the internet for what this page explains.

No accounts of its own

SimOs does not ask you to sign up, give an email or sign in, and there are no user profiles on any SimOs server. The only accounts it uses are yours, on the services you choose to link (section 4).

On your computer

Your projects, their files, screenshots, videos, settings, the recent list (folders and addresses you open) and a technical log with no personal data (registro.txt, with “Open the log” in the app) are kept on your PC. SimOs does not receive them. To open a folder, SimOs serves it with a local server that only answers your own computer (127.0.0.1) and is not visible from the network. If your project comes with a development server (for example npm run dev), SimOs shows you the command and only runs it if you say yes.

If you open a web address, SimOs loads it as a browser would: that website receives your IP address and the identifier of the simulated device, and handles them under its own policy.

Your keys (the App Store Connect key, the GitHub and Codemagic tokens) are stored only in Windows Credential Manager. They are not in your project files and do not pass through any SimOs server.

1. Free 7-day trial and the computer fingerprint

The first 7 days of SimOs Pro are free. So that reinstalling does not renew the trial, SimOs sends a SimOs server, hosted on Supabase (the “simos-prueba” function), this data:

  • a cryptographic fingerprint (SHA-256) of the computer's identifier mixed with a salt of SimOs's own: the plain identifier never leaves your PC and cannot be recovered from the fingerprint;
  • the platform (windows) and a random one-time number (so the reply cannot be reused).

The server stores the fingerprint, the platform and the date of the first time, and replies with that date signed. It stores no names, emails or accounts. The function's code does not log the IP address, headers or browser; like any hosting, Supabase may process the IP to deliver the reply, under its privacy policy. Without a connection there is no trial (days are never given away): the rest of SimOs keeps working.

2. Anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics 4)

The first time, SimOs asks whether you allow usage statistics. Without your yes nothing is sent. You can change it in Settings: when you withdraw permission, the event queue and the identifier are deleted.

If you allow them, SimOs sends Google Analytics 4 (stream “SimOs Windows”, measurement ID G-SWV544QV13, through the Measurement Protocol, no cookies) events such as opening the app, opening a folder or an address (only the type, never the path or the address), starting a development server, using the official simulator, taking a screenshot, recording a video, building, and the Pro purchase with its amount and currency. Each event carries the device or mode used, a session identifier, the SimOs version, the app language and the download channel (Microsoft Store or itch.io). The installation identifier is a random number. No paths, folder names, addresses, project contents or personal data are sent, and Google is told not to use them for advertising. As with any internet connection, Google sees the IP address it is connected from and handles it under its privacy policy.

3. Tool downloads

SimOs does not bundle other authors' programs: it downloads them when needed, with your permission, and checks each download against its checksum (SHA-256) before using it; if it does not match, it deletes it. In those downloads the source server sees your IP address, and SimOs sends it no data of yours:

  • Phone engine (WebKit): the first time you use it (176 MB), from Playwright's download servers.
  • Your iPhone over USB: go-ios and pymobiledevice3 (GitHub and PyPI), and ffmpeg on Windows (GitHub), to view the iPhone and prepare the store video. Everything with the iPhone is done over the cable, on your PC: nothing you see on it, nor its logs or crash reports, goes to the internet.
  • Android: if you build for Android and something is missing, and you accept, SimOs downloads the Temurin JDK (Adoptium) and the Android components from Google.

4. Services you link

Only if you choose to, to build an iPhone app without a Mac, SimOs talks directly from your PC to these services, with your accounts and credentials:

  • GitHub: linked with a personal token or with GitHub's device code. SimOs creates a private repository if you have none, uploads your project (never forcing anything), writes the workflow .github/workflows/simos-ios.yml and stores in that repository's secrets, encrypted with its public key, the App Store Connect key and the signing team, so GitHub can build and sign. It starts the build and follows its progress. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Codemagic: with your personal token, SimOs starts builds in your account (up to 500 free minutes a month), creates a group of encrypted variables with the App Store Connect key and downloads the signed .ipa to your PC. See Codemagic's privacy policy.
  • App Store Connect (Apple): with the API key you create in your developer account (the .p8 file with its Key ID and Issuer ID), SimOs signs the requests on your PC, checks your app and uploads the build to TestFlight. It can also use Xcode Cloud if you set it up once on a Mac. See Apple's privacy policy.

SimOs never turns on billing for any service, and before starting it warns you if a private GitHub repository is going to use paid minutes. You can unlink each account whenever you like and delete the GitHub and Codemagic tokens and the Apple key from the app itself; the secrets already in your repository or your Codemagic account you delete from their own website.

5. Purchases and subscription

SimOs Pro (€1.99 a month or €14.99 a year in the euro area, with 7 days free) is paid through the Microsoft Store, which handles the payment. SimOs asks the Store, from your PC, whether your subscription is active, and does not receive your payment details. The itch.io copy does not sell Pro: there only the 7-day trial works.

6. Unlocking with an ad through a QR code (off in this version)

SimOs is prepared so that, without Pro, Capture, Record and Build unlock by watching a rewarded ad in the PureVibeCode iPhone app, through PureVibeCode's unlock server (“purevibe-desbloqueo”, also on Supabase). In version 1.0 that path is off: SimOs does not connect to that server or show ads, and those features are Pro-only (or trial). When it is turned on, the computer will send a fingerprint of a secret of this installation, the type of unlock and the technical data of the request, with the same safeguards explained in PureVibeCode's privacy policy (no names, emails or IP address), and this policy will be updated first. “See it on your iPhone” (opening your project on the iPhone over the same Wi-Fi) is not in this version either.

What we do not do

  • We do not use your projects, screenshots or keys for advertising, and SimOs shows no ads.
  • We do not take your code off your PC, except to the services you link (section 4).
  • We do not track your activity in other apps or on third-party websites.
  • We do not sell or give your data to anyone, and we do not create user profiles.

Your rights

You can ask for access, rectification, deletion or restriction of the data SimOs processes, and object to certain processing, by writing to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com. Since the computer fingerprint is not tied to your name, to find it we will need your help identifying it; if it is deleted, the 7-day trial would start again. The data on your PC is under your control: you can delete it from the app or by uninstalling it. For what GitHub, Codemagic, Apple, Microsoft or Google hold, ask that company. If you believe we do not handle your data as we should, you can complain to the data protection authority of your country (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos).

Children

SimOs is a tool for people who build apps and is not aimed at children under 13; we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Languages and changes to this policy

SimOs is shown in seven languages; this policy is in Spanish and English and applies to all of them. If what the app does changes, this page is updated and the new date is shown above.
