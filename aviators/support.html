Help and support

Privacy policy · Terms of use

What you can do here

  • Questions, bugs or suggestions: write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com. Include your device model, OS version and what happened.
  • You bought something and do not have it: open Settings or the Canteen and tap “Restore purchases”. If it still does not show up, write to us with the store receipt (Apple or Microsoft; the Order ID or transaction identifier).
  • Delete your account and data: Settings → Account and privacy → “Delete my account”. More details in the Privacy policy.
  • Change ad consent: Settings → Account and privacy → Privacy and consent → “Manage ad consent” (where the law requires it); and Apple's tracking, in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Mac and PC there are no ads, so there is no consent to change.
  • Cancel the Ace Club subscription (if you have it): on iPhone and iPad, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on a Mac, App Store → your account → Subscriptions; on PC (Microsoft Store), at account.microsoft.com/services → Manage → Cancel subscription.
  • Your save: it is stored only on your device; this version does not make cloud copies. The anonymous account also lives on the device: if you uninstall the game or start on another device, you start with a new account. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, permanent purchases and the subscription come back with “Restore purchases”; on PC, reinstalling creates a new account and that button does not recover what you bought in the Microsoft Store. If you had unspent purchased Funds, write to us with the order number of the purchase.
  • Mac and PC: the Mac version is bought in the Mac App Store and the PC version in the Microsoft Store. The PC version downloaded from itch.io does not buy inside: its “Buy” will open the game’s page in the Microsoft Store once the game is published there, and it restores nothing. To use what you bought in the Microsoft Store, install the Microsoft Store version.

We reply within 3 working days at most.
