Aviators: Sky Wars

Privacy policy

Terms of use · Help and support

Summary: Aviators: Sky Wars is an air-combat game where you fly alone against the AI. It does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, camera or microphone. To verify your purchases and credit the Funds you buy, the game creates an anonymous account (a random identifier with no personal data). Only the iPhone and iPad version has ads (Google AdMob) and analytics (Firebase Analytics), which depend on your consent where the law requires it; if the platform says you are a minor, ads are not personalized and no tracking permission is asked. The Mac and PC versions have no ads and no analytics. You can delete your account from inside the game.

Who is responsible

Gabriel Micó Victoria, developer of Aviators: Sky Wars. For any question about your data or to exercise your rights, write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com.

Anonymous account, save and purchases

  • No sign-up: when you open the game, an anonymous session is created on our server. There is no username or password. The identifier is stored on your device.
  • Game save: your progress is stored only on your device: this version of the game does not upload your save to any server or make cloud copies. If you uninstall the game, the save is deleted with it. There is no chat, no messages and no content from other players: you play alone against the AI.
  • Funds and purchases: purchases are charged by the store (Apple or Microsoft, depending on where you buy); we receive the notice that it happened, with the transaction data that credits it (through RevenueCat on iPhone and iPad, and directly from the game on Mac and PC; the refund notices Apple sends for Mac purchases reach us forwarded by RevenueCat) and record the purchased Funds in a ledger so they are credited and neither lost nor counted twice. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Rewarded videos (iPhone and iPad only): AdMob tells our server that you watched a video (with the anonymous identifier and an operation code) so the reward can be delivered.
  • Mac and PC: on a Mac, Apple charges the purchase and the game sends our server the transaction signed by Apple to credit the Funds; in the Windows version from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft charges it and the game notifies our server with a purchase identifier.

Ads

On iPhone and iPad the game is free and shows Google AdMob ads, with no interstitial ads:

  • App-open ad: on one of every five valid app openings, never during the first days and never right after a purchase or a video. Any purchase removes it for good.
  • Rewarded videos: most are optional: you watch them by choice to earn an extra. If you have not bought anything, you also watch one to collect the Field Crate, the daily reward, the Squadron Trunks and the free-track pass rewards. Without a video, the reward waits.

Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, before requesting any ad the game shows Google's consent form. On iPhone and iPad it also asks for Apple's tracking permission (ATT), with an explanation first. If you accept, Google may show personalized ads and use the advertising identifier. If you decline, you will see non-personalized ads and the game works exactly the same. You can change it any time: on iPhone and iPad in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and in the game under Settings → Account and privacy → Privacy and consent → “Manage ad consent” (where the law requires it).

We limit ad content to “parental guidance” (PG) or stricter and block sensitive categories.

Mac and PC: these versions have no ads: no app-open ad, no banner and no videos. So there is no consent form and no tracking permission in them, and the rewards that on iPhone and iPad are collected after watching a video wait until you buy something (with any purchase they are collected directly).

Minors and age signals

Aviators: Sky Wars is not directed at children and its age rating is 13+ (cartoon and fantasy violence between planes, no blood; shot-down pilots bail out by parachute). The game does not ask your age with a screen of its own. Instead, on iPhone and iPad, if your device supports it, it checks the age range that Apple (Declared Age Range, iOS 26 or later) already holds, and receives only a range (for example “13 to 15”), never your date of birth. You can choose not to share it: the game works the same and behaves as usual.

If the range says you are under 18: ads are not personalized, you are not asked for tracking permission, you are not shown personalized offers and purchases have a low monthly spending limit. If it says under 13: ads also get child-directed treatment (with content suitable for all audiences) and device analytics are turned off. If you believe a minor has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Mac and PC: in these versions the game does not check any age signal, so the spending limit for minors does not apply there.

Usage analytics

  • What is collected: Firebase Analytics automatic events (for example, opening the game and sessions), with no personal data; a Firebase installation identifier; country, language, OS version and device model. They are used only to count how the game is used.
  • iPhone and iPad only: Firebase Analytics is only in the iPhone and iPad version (there is no analytics on Mac and PC); it is not used for advertising or profiling.
  • What is not collected: the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used for analytics.
  • Consent: analytics are off by default and are only turned on once the consent form is resolved (or, outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, where it is not required), and you are not under 13.

Notifications

On iPhone and iPad the game asks for permission to send you notifications (for example, when your Field Crate is ready). They are few, never about purchases, and you can turn them off in Settings or in the system settings. Event and season notifications are a separate switch that starts off. On Mac and PC the game does not offer notifications.

How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you uninstall the game or delete your account from Settings.
  • On our server: the anonymous account, the Funds balance and the purchase ledger, while you keep your account. Video verification records are deleted after 2 days and technical alerts after 180 days.
  • When you delete your account: your account and its data on our server are removed, and the accounting records of your purchases are kept without any identifier of yours (kept only because of the legal accounting obligation).
  • Third parties (Google, RevenueCat, Apple): according to their own policies.

How to delete your account and data

  1. In the game: Settings → Account and privacy → “Delete my account” (it asks for two confirmations). Your account is deleted from the server and all game data on the device is erased, and the game starts over as a fresh install.
  2. Without access to the game: write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com from any email address, giving the Order ID on the Apple receipt of a purchase (if you made one), and we will delete your account. If you have no Apple purchase, the account can only be deleted from the game. We reply within 30 days at most.

Deleting the account does not cancel subscriptions (they are cancelled with Apple or Microsoft, see the Terms of use) and does not refund purchases. Unspent purchased Funds are lost when the account is deleted.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability (GDPR), and to withdraw your consent at any time (without affecting what was done before). Write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or the authority in your country.

Legal basis: the account and purchases are processed to provide the service you asked for; personalized ads and analytics, with your consent; non-personalized ads, under legitimate interest in funding the free version; accounting ledgers, under a legal obligation.

International transfers and security

Google, RevenueCat, Apple and Microsoft may process data outside the European Economic Area, under the safeguards of their own policies (standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework). All communications are encrypted (HTTPS). Server tables do not accept direct writes: they are only changed through functions that validate each value.
