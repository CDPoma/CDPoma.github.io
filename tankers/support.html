Help and support

Privacy policy · Terms of use

What you can do here

  • Questions, bugs or suggestions: write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com. Include your device (iPhone, iPad, Mac or PC and its model), OS version, where you installed the game (App Store, Microsoft Store or itch.io) and what happened.
  • You bought something and do not have it: open Settings or the Shop and tap “Restore purchases”, with the same store account you bought with (Apple ID or Microsoft account). If it still does not show up, write to us with the order number on the Apple receipt (Order ID) or the Microsoft one, and the date of the purchase.
  • You play on PC with the itch.io version: that version sells nothing; “Buy” opens the game's page in the Microsoft Store. What you buy in the Microsoft Store is used in the version installed from the Microsoft Store.
  • Delete your account and data: Settings → Account and privacy → “Delete my account”, on any platform. More details in the Privacy policy.
  • Change ad consent (iPhone and iPad): Settings → Account and privacy → Privacy and consent → “Manage ad consent” (where the law requires it); and Apple's tracking, in your iPhone or iPad Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. There are no ads on Mac and PC.
  • Cancel the Supreme Command subscription (if you have it): on iPhone and iPad, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Mac, App Store → your name → Account Settings → Subscriptions → Manage; on PC, at account.microsoft.com/services → Manage → Cancel subscription. Deleting the game account does not cancel it.
  • Your game save: it is stored only on your device; this version makes no cloud copies. The anonymous account also lives on the device: if you uninstall the game or start on another device, you start with a new account. Permanent purchases and the subscription come back with “Restore purchases”; if you had purchased Funds left, write to us with the order number of the purchase and we will look into it.

We reply within 3 working days at most.
