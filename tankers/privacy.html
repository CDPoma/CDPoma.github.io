Tankers: Steel Wars

Privacy policy

Terms of use · Help and support

Summary: Tankers: Steel Wars is a tank game where you play alone against the AI, on iPhone, iPad, Mac and PC (Windows). It does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or microphone. The camera is used only on the iPhone, if you ask for it, to read the QR code the computer shows, and it saves no image. To verify your purchases and credit the Funds you buy, the game creates an anonymous account (a random identifier, without your name, email or phone number). Only the iPhone and iPad version has ads (Google AdMob) and analytics (Firebase Analytics), which depend on your consent where the law requires it; if the platform says you are a minor, ads are not personalized and no tracking permission is asked. The Mac and PC versions have no ads on their screen and no analytics: if you want to watch a reward's video from the computer, you watch it on your iPhone by scanning its QR code (all platforms). You can delete your account from inside the game, on any platform.

Who is responsible

Anonymous account, game save and purchases

  • No sign-up (all platforms): when you open the game, an anonymous session is created on our server. There is no username or password. The identifier is stored on your device, so if you uninstall the game or install it on another device, you start with a new account.
  • Game save (all platforms): your progress is stored only on your device: this version of the game does not upload your save to any server or make cloud copies. If you uninstall the game, the save is deleted with it; permanent purchases and the subscription come back with “Restore purchases”. There is no chat, no messages and no content from other players: you play alone against the AI.
  • Purchases on iPhone and iPad: charged by Apple; RevenueCat tells our server they happened and we record what you bought in a ledger so it is delivered and neither lost nor counted twice.
  • Purchases on Mac: charged by Apple. The game sends our server the transaction signed by Apple, and the server checks the signature, the game, the product and that the purchase belongs to your anonymous account before delivering it.
  • Purchases on PC: in the Microsoft Store version they are charged by Microsoft; the game sends our server the product's Store identifier and a random identifier for that purchase, and the server records it (with daily limits against abuse). Microsoft does not tell us the amount. The itch.io version sells nothing.
  • Payment details (all platforms): we never receive them, nor your Apple ID or your Microsoft account.
  • Refunds: when Apple notifies us of a refund (iPhone, iPad and Mac), we record it in the ledger and the refunded Funds are deducted. The Microsoft Store does not notify our server of refunds. See the “Terms of use”.
  • Rewarded videos (iPhone and iPad): AdMob tells our server that you watched a video (with the anonymous identifier and an operation code) so the reward can be delivered.
  • Computer videos (Mac and PC): the computer shows a QR code. In Tankers on your iPhone, go to Settings → “Scan the computer's QR” to read it: the camera is used only to read that code, and no image is saved or sent. You then watch the video on your iPhone, with AdMob, and the computer receives the confirmation from our server (see “What leaves your device”).
  • Steel Chests (all platforms): those in the paid season-pass track give random rewards (blueprints or Funds). The odds are shown in the game when you view the chest and when you open it.

Ads

iPhone and iPad only. The game is free and on iPhone and iPad it shows Google AdMob ads in two formats, with no interstitial ads:

  • App-open ad: on one of every five valid app openings, never during the first days and never right after a purchase or a video. Any purchase removes it for good.
  • Rewarded videos: you watch them to earn an extra. They are optional, except for collecting the Field Crate, the daily entry reward, the Steel Chests and the free season-pass track: players who have not bought anything watch one to collect them. If no video is available, the reward is not collected and waits.

Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, before requesting any ad the game shows Google's consent form. It then asks for Apple's tracking permission (ATT), with an explanation first. If you accept, Google may show personalized ads and use the advertising identifier. If you decline, you will see non-personalized ads and the game works exactly the same. You can change it any time: in your iPhone or iPad Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and in the game under Settings → Account and privacy → Privacy and consent → “Manage ad consent” (where the law requires it).

The game limits ad content to “parental guidance” (PG) or stricter, and to “general audiences” (G) if the platform says you are under 13.

Mac and PC: they show no ads on their screen, do not ask for tracking permission and send nothing to Google. If you have made a purchase, reward videos are collected directly. If not, the computer shows a QR code and the video is watched on your iPhone (see “Anonymous account, game save and purchases”); without an iPhone to hand, the reward waits.

Minors and age signals

Tankers: Steel Wars is not directed at children and its age rating is 13+ (cartoon and fantasy violence between vehicles, no blood). The game does not ask your age with a screen of its own. Instead, on iPhone and iPad, if your device supports it, it checks the age range Apple already holds (Declared Age Range, iOS 26 or later) and receives only a range (for example “13 to 15”), never your date of birth. You can choose not to share it: the game works the same and behaves as usual.

If the range says you are under 18: ads are not personalized, you are not asked for tracking permission, you are not shown personalized offers and purchases have a low monthly spending limit. If it says under 13: ads also get child-directed treatment (with content suitable for all audiences) and device analytics are turned off. On Mac and PC the game does not check any age signal (there are no ads or analytics there). If you believe a minor has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Usage analytics

  • Where: only in the iPhone and iPad version. Mac and PC have no analytics.
  • What is collected: the automatic events that Firebase Analytics records by itself (first open, sessions, app updates and, where applicable, in-app purchases), with no name, email or advertising identifier, and no game-specific events; a Firebase installation identifier; country, language, OS version and device model. They are used only to count how the game is used.
  • What is not collected: the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used for analytics.
  • Consent: analytics are off by default and are only turned on once the consent form is resolved (or, outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, where it is not required), and you are not under 13.

Notifications

On iPhone and iPad, the game asks for permission to send you notifications (for example, when your Field Crate is ready). They are few, never about purchases, and you can turn them off in Settings or in the system settings. Event and season notifications are a separate switch that starts off. Notifications are local: they are scheduled by the device itself. There are no remote notifications and no token is stored for sending them. On Mac and PC the game sends no notifications.

How long data is kept

  • Computer QR requests (Mac and PC): they are deleted once they have been expired for more than one day, and video confirmations after 7 days. They store no data from your account or your iPhone, so there is nothing to link them to when you delete your account.
  • On your device (all platforms): until you uninstall the game or delete your account from Settings.
  • On our server: the anonymous account, the Funds balance and the purchase ledger, until you delete your account. They are not deleted automatically for inactivity: if you uninstall the game without deleting the account, they stay on the server, with no data that would let anyone know they are yours. If you want them gone, delete the account from the game before uninstalling it. Video verification records are deleted after 2 days and technical alerts (for example, about unusual purchases) after 180 days.
  • When you delete your account: your account (with its Funds balance) and your video records are removed. The accounting records of your purchases are kept, only because of the legal accounting obligation, unlinked from your account; the internal key of the records of Funds spending and of permanent purchases on PC keeps the anonymous identifier, which is no longer linked to any game data. Technical alerts that mention your anonymous identifier are deleted automatically after 180 days. The anonymous sign-in identifier stays in the authentication system, with no game data linked to it.
  • Third parties (Google, RevenueCat, Apple, Microsoft, Unity): according to their own policies.

How to delete your account and data

  1. In the game, on any platform: Settings → Account and privacy → “Delete my account” (it asks for two confirmations). Your account is deleted from the server and all game data on the device is erased, and the game starts over as a fresh install.
  2. Without access to the game: write to gabrielmicovictoria@gmail.com from any email address. Because the account is anonymous, we can only find it from an Apple purchase (iPhone, iPad or Mac): give the order number (Order ID) on the Apple receipt and we will delete the account it was delivered to. Without an Apple purchase there is no data that lets us know which account is yours (Microsoft Store purchases are recorded without any data from your Microsoft account or the order), so the account can only be deleted from the game. We reply within 30 days at most.

Deleting the account does not cancel subscriptions (they are cancelled with Apple or Microsoft, see the Terms of use) and does not refund purchases. Unspent purchased Funds are lost when the account is deleted.
