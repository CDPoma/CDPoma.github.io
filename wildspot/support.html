WildSpot · Privacy policy

Respect nature: do not eat, touch or pick anything you are not sure about, respect private land and do not uproot or disturb species that may be protected. The AI can be wrong: it is not a field guide or an expert. If a bug stings or bites, do not get close: use the zoom.

WildSpot identifies fruit trees, trees, shrubs, plants, flowers, cacti, lizards, bugs and bees from a photo with AI, and saves them as finds with their location on a private map: only your friends see them. Here are the most common questions.

Frequently asked questions

How do I identify a plant or a bug?

Tap the camera button in the centre of the bar, then "Take photo" or "Choose from gallery". Make sure the key parts show: leaves, flower or fruit on a plant; the whole bug, up close. The app gives you the common and scientific name, the category and the confidence in %, what it looked at and what it can be mistaken for. If the confidence is low, the app says so: do not rely on that result. If the AI gets it wrong, fix the name and category before you save.

Why does an ad show while the photo is analysed?

The button says only "Take photo" (or "Choose from gallery") and, under it, in small text, "Includes a short ad": when the photo is done a rewarded video ad plays while the AI analyses it, never before you take it. The result is shown once the reward is earned. Everyone sees it, Pro included. If the ad is closed early or no ad is available, the analysed photo stays pending: without Pro you can try again, go Pro or open "Ad privacy options" (where required); with Pro, only try again. WildSpot Pro removes the banner and the app-open ad, not this short ad. With or without Pro, there is a limit of 40 identifications per day per account.

How do I set my name? Do I need an account?

There is no email or password. The map opens without a name; to identify, see the feed, save finds and have friends, the app asks for a display name (2 to 30 characters) and for you to accept the community guidelines. Your account lives on this device: if you delete the app, you start from scratch.

How do I add friends?

In Profile, Friends section, you will find your 6-character code: share it or type your friend's in "Add by code" and tap "Send request". You are friends when the other person accepts. From Profile you can also remove friends, block someone and change your code if someone you do not want has it.

Who sees my finds and where they are?

Only you and your friends; there is no public feed. When you save a find you choose "Only me" or "My friends" and, separately, the switch "My friends see where it is". With the place hidden, your friends see the photo, name, note, likes and comments, but not the place or the point on the map. You can change both later from the find's ⋯ menu; what they have already seen may have been copied. If the AI thinks it may be a protected species, the place starts hidden.

How do I like and comment?

In the feed, tap the ❤ on a card, or open the find to read and write comments. Only people who can see that find can do it. You can delete your own comments, and the owner of a find can delete any comment on it. The Activity bell tells you about likes, comments and friend requests.

How do the map and "Go" work?

The map is Apple's, with satellite imagery and a 3D view. The chips on top turn each category on or off, and the round button has Search (coordinates or the name of your finds), Layers (Mine, From friends, Towns and roads) and 3D. Press and hold a point to save a new find there or head there. "Go" opens a map with a compass showing bearing and distance, or "Directions by car".

I saved a find without a place and it does not show on the map

With no location there is no point on the map, and the place cannot be added later. A gallery photo has no place unless you tap "I am where the photo was taken"; if your location was not available, the app asks whether to save it without a place. Also check that location permission is on in the iPhone Settings.

I bought Pro and it does not show

Go to Feed → your avatar → ⚙ → "See WildSpot Pro" → "Restore purchases". Sometimes the store takes a few minutes to confirm the payment. If it still does not show, write to us with your Apple receipt.

How do I cancel the Pro subscription?

It is managed from your Apple account: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → WildSpot Pro.

What if a find or a comment is inappropriate?

Tap "More options" (⋯) on the find and choose "Report find" (dangerous information, offensive content, spam or another reason), or "Report" on the comment. What you report stops showing for you at once, and with 3 reports from different people it is hidden for everyone; the developer reviews it within 24 hours. You can also block its author: you stop being friends and see nothing of theirs; you can unblock them in Profile → Friends.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Feed → your avatar → ⚙ and tap "Delete my account". Your name, finds with their photos and places, comments and friendships are deleted. It cannot be undone.

The app says "Update WildSpot"

There is a new version with important changes. Tap the button to open the App Store and update to keep using it.

Where the data comes from

  • Map and satellite imagery: Apple Maps (MapKit).
  • Name, category, warnings and fruiting months: given by the AI (Claude, by Anthropic) from your photo; they are only a guide and may be wrong.
  • Finds, photos, likes and comments: those of the people who use WildSpot, only for their friends.
